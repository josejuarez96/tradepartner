- Paper reconciliation no longer halts the book on a stock dividend: the broker whole-share credit plus its cash-in-lieu reconcile, booked as a `cash_in_lieu` adjustment.
### Fixed
- #1626 (#127) stored an Alpaca stock dividend as a `split` action of its rate, so the ledger grew a held position by a fractional number of shares while the broker credits whole shares (floor(shares x ratio)) plus a `CIL` (cash in lieu of stock, Alpaca's `CIL`) activity for the remainder; reconciliation flagged the unknown `CIL` type and the sub-share position difference and halted. Schema version 27 (additive) adds the `adjustments.kind` `cash_in_lieu`; `execution/reconcile.py` seats a `CIL` for a held stock dividend (a split with `1 < ratio <= alpaca.stock_dividend_max_rate`, `ex_date <= S`): it books one `cash_in_lieu` adjustment (negative fractional `quantity`, the broker's `net_amount`, keyed by the CIL id) when the name holds a stock dividend and the amount matches the expected cash (the fractional remainder valued at the post-ex price = the reference price / the split ratio) within a new frozen `risk.cash_in_lieu_tolerance` (relative, default 0.10), accepted from the ex-date through a new `alpaca.cash_in_lieu_max_lag_sessions` (default 10) sessions (the payable date); `execution/ledger.from_journal` floors the issuer's exact share position for the row so a whole-share exit never strands a share. An unmatched `CIL`, one beyond the tolerance or the lag window, on a name with no stock dividend that session, beside a broker quantity that is not the whole-share floor, or a second `CIL` id on one name, stays `unexplained_activity` and halts, as any residual does. paper-trading spec req 6 and ADR 0010 amended; the frozen `risk.cash_in_lieu_tolerance` requires schema 27, so it must not land while a window frozen before it (lacking the key) is open.
