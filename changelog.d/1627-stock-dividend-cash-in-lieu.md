- Paper reconciliation no longer halts the book on a stock dividend: the broker whole-share credit plus its cash-in-lieu reconcile, booked as a `cash_in_lieu` adjustment.
### Fixed
- #1626 (#127) stored an Alpaca stock dividend as a `split` action of its rate, so the ledger grew a held position by a fractional number of shares while the broker credits whole shares (floor(shares x ratio)) plus a `CIL` (cash in lieu of stock) activity for the remainder; reconciliation flagged the unknown `CIL` type and the sub-share position difference and halted. Schema version 27 (additive) adds the `adjustments.kind` `cash_in_lieu`; `execution/reconcile.py` seats a `CIL` for a held stock dividend (a split with `1 < ratio <= alpaca.stock_dividend_max_rate`, `ex_date <= S`): it books one `cash_in_lieu` adjustment (negative fractional `quantity`, the broker `net_amount`, keyed by the CIL id) matched against the expected cash (fractional remainder x the reference price) within a new frozen `risk.cash_in_lieu_tolerance` (relative, default 0.10), and rounds the expected position to whole shares. An unmatched `CIL`, one beyond the tolerance, or one on a name with no stock dividend that session stays `unexplained_activity` and halts, as any residual does. Requires schema 27, so it must not land while a window frozen before it (lacking `risk.cash_in_lieu_tolerance`) is open. paper-trading spec req 6 amended.
