# Spec: Strategy lab (sweeps, cadence as a parameter, shadow-paper replay)

**Status:** Draft  ·  **Issue:** #281  ·  **Related:** [backtest spec](backtest.md) (amended below), [Phase 3 plan](../plans/backtest.md), [paper-trading spec](paper-trading.md) (reqs 3 to 8, 10), [ADR 0005](../decisions/0005-objective-benchmark-stop-criteria.md) (unchanged), [ADR 0006](../decisions/0006-universe-and-cadence.md) (amended below), [ADR 0009](../decisions/0009-price-vendor.md) (history from 2016-01-04), [ADR 0010](../decisions/0010-phase-4-risk-rules.md) (the replay runs under its rules), [H1](../hypotheses/h1-momentum-12-1.md), research [G1](../research/2026-09-25-g1-momentum-post-2010.md), [G3](../research/2026-09-25-trend-timing.md), [G4](../research/2026-09-25-g4-signal-combination.md), [trial registry](../research/trial-registry.md), STATUS "Decisions needed" (rebalance cadence), development-process domain rules 1 to 3

## Problem & why now

Phase 3 registers and runs one hypothesis at a time: a file, a registration, a trial. That is right for H1 and wrong for what comes next. While H1's six-month paper run proceeds (Phase 4), the owner wants to develop strategies in volume: hundreds to thousands of registered runs, rebalance cadences faster than monthly, and a fast simulated execution replay to exercise the Phase 4 order path over history. Done carelessly, volume is exactly what the charter warns against (principle 2: count every trial, keep a locked holdout; principle 6: around 100 trades is noise). Done as this spec says, every variant of every sweep is a registered trial in the family's N, the holdout is never touched by a sweep, a variant leaves a sweep only through a pre-declared selection rule with a logged owner decision, and the replay is labelled a simulation everywhere it appears.

Three things change. **Sweeps:** one file registers a grid of variants, each its own hypothesis and its own trials, with the expected magnitude and retirement condition pre-registered per sweep. **Cadence:** the rebalance schedule becomes a frozen hypothesis parameter (`month_end`, `week_end`, `daily`), which resolves the pending Phase 3 amendment in STATUS "Decisions needed" and amends ADR 0006's cadence sentence. **Replay:** a harness runs a registered hypothesis through the Phase 4 execution layer against a scripted fake broker over history, producing fill-timing and cost distributions and exercising halts and reconciliation, as a test and calibration asset that is never evidence toward ADR 0005's paper criterion.

The registry's four guarantees stay intact and are restated where each requirement touches them: the holdout is locked; the holdout is spent at most once per hypothesis, by the owner; every run is a registered trial; the deflated Sharpe is computed over the whole family.

## Users & usage

- **The owner** writes a sweep file, merges it, runs `tradepartner sweep register`, then `tradepartner sweep run` on the real store (only the owner runs anything against the real store, CLAUDE.md rule 9), reads the sweep report and the family view, and promotes at most `lab.max_promotions_per_sweep` variants per sweep with `tradepartner sweep promote --reason`. The owner runs `tradepartner lab replay` on a temp-file copy of the store the command makes for it. Nothing here places an order.
- **The `backtest-runner` agent** (Phase 3 T45) runs sweeps and replays **only on a temp-file or fixture store** (`store_path` set explicitly; synthetic trials), never on the real store, and never passes a holdout, gap or promotion flag. The sweep runner has none to pass.
- **Phase 4** is untouched: H1's paper window is monthly, `paper start` refuses a hypothesis whose cadence is not `month_end` (req 11), and no replay row is read by `paper report` or `paper check`.
- **Tests**, on every PR, register and run a four-variant fixture sweep on the fixture store, run the cadence suites at all three cadences, and replay the fixture hypothesis against the scripted fake with the network disabled.

## Definitions

- **Family**: as in the backtest spec: one of the enumerated `hypotheses.families`, the unit for N, V and holdout spends. This spec does not change what a family is. Issue #281's "family of hypotheses from one file" is a **sweep** below.
- **Sweep**: a file under `docs/sweeps/<slug>.md` (template `docs/templates/sweep.md`) that names one family, one holdout, one `in_sample_start`, a fixed parameter block and a **grid**, plus the prose a hypothesis file carries (rationale, expected magnitude, prior-evidence disclosure, retirement condition), written once **per sweep, not per variant**. Registered as a `sweeps` row plus one `hypotheses` row per variant.
- **Grid**: a table of dotted config keys, each with a list of values, restricted to `lab.sweepable_keys`. The **variants** are the Cartesian product of the lists, at most `lab.max_variants_per_sweep`. The **canonical order** of variants is ascending `params_sha256` of each variant's frozen set; the owner never chooses it. A variant's slug is `<sweep-slug>--v<NNN>` (NNN its 1-based canonical index, zero-padded to three digits).
- **Variant**: one registered hypothesis whose `hypotheses` row is linked to a sweep through `sweep_variants`. It carries the sweep's holdout and window. A variant is never run by `backtest`, never spends the holdout and is never promoted in place (req 4 and 5).
- **Read group**: the variants of one sweep that agree on every frozen key except `strategy.formation_months`, `strategy.skip_months`, `strategy.top_fraction`, `strategy.weighting`, `schedule.signal_anchor` and `execution.fill_price` (the keys that change the computation but not the provider reads). One read set per step serves the whole group (req 2).
- **Selection statistic**: the sweep file's `selection_statistic`, one of `dsr_excess`, `sharpe_annual_excess_spy`, `excess_cagr_spy`, always read at the **base** cost level from the variant's latest `ok` in-sample trial over the sweep window. **Complete sweep**: every variant has an `ok`, non-synthetic, in-sample trial over the sweep's default window under the sweep's current registration.
- **Promotion**: an owner decision (`owner_decisions.kind = promotion`) that registers a standalone hypothesis file whose frozen parameters equal one variant's, with a sweep-provenance section, so that the holdout can be spent on it under the Phase 3 rules (req 4).
- **Family holdout**: the union of the frozen holdouts of every registered hypothesis in the family: `[min holdout.start, max holdout.end]`. It only grows (req 5).
- **Cadence**: the frozen `schedule.rebalance_cadence` ∈ {`month_end`, `week_end`, `daily`}. **Rebalance session** T_i: under `month_end` the last XNYS session of each calendar month (the Phase 3 definition, unchanged); under `week_end` the last XNYS session of each ISO week (Monday to Sunday); under `daily` every XNYS session. All three come from `tradepartner.calendar` over `all_sessions()`, never from weekday arithmetic. **Fill session** F_i = `calendar.next_session(T_i)` and **read time** close(T_i) are unchanged at every cadence. **Hold**: one period, from F_i to F_{i+1}. `daily` is the floor: the store holds daily bars, so the finest schedule is one rebalance per session, read at close(T_i), filled at F_i's `execution.fill_price`; nothing intraday exists or is specified (out of scope without a data ADR).
- **Period** i: the interval (T_i, T_{i+1}] between consecutive rebalance sessions. **Period i return**: equity at close(T_{i+1}) over equity at close(T_i), minus one. Period returns are the unit for Sharpe, skew, kurtosis, the DSR's T and the ADR 0005 tracking check (monthly, since paper stays monthly). **`PERIODS_PER_YEAR`** = {`month_end`: 12, `week_end`: 52, `daily`: 252} is a constant table in `backtest/schedule.py` derived from the cadence, not config; `MONTHS_PER_YEAR = 12` is its `month_end` entry.
- **Signal anchor**: the frozen `schedule.signal_anchor` ∈ {`month_end`, `offset`}. It fixes the two bars the momentum score reads at T (req 7). `month_end` is the Phase 3 rule and H1's.
- **Replay**: one execution of `tradepartner lab replay` over a session window of a registered hypothesis on a **temp-file copy** of the store, driving the Phase 4 tracking run (`execution.run.tracking_run`) with a **replay clock** and a **scripted broker**. **Fill model**: the `replay.*` keys that turn a bar into a fill (reference price, slippage draw, partial-fill, expiry and rejection probabilities, seed). **Fault script**: a TOML file listing (session, event) pairs the scripted broker or clock acts out (`reject`, `expire`, `partial`, `vanish`, `clock_late`, `clock_skew`, `duplicate_id`, `foreign_position`) and the session on which the replay issues its scripted `paper resume`.
- **Detail level**: what a trial stores beyond metrics and rebalance rows: `full` (the Phase 3 tables as written: daily equity per series per cost level, base-level weights) or `summary` (daily equity for every series at the base level only, equity at rebalance sessions for the other levels, no `trial_weights`). Metrics are computed from the full in-memory result at both levels (req 14).

## Requirements

1. **Sweep file and registration.** `tradepartner sweep register <file>` parses the single ```` ```toml sweep ```` block: `slug` (= file stem), `family` (from `hypotheses.families`; `oracle` refused on the real store as for a hypothesis), `title`, `in_sample_start`, `[holdout]` `start`/`end`, a fixed block naming **every** `strategy.*` and `costs.*` key and `schedule.rebalance_cadence` (either as a fixed value or as a grid axis; a key in both is refused), any other frozen key it pins, `[grid]` (dotted key → non-empty list of distinct values; every key in `lab.sweepable_keys`, so a `costs.*`, `universe.*`, `holdout.*`, `gap.*`, `adjust.*`, `master.*`, `metrics.*`, `backtest.*`, `benchmarks` or `alpaca.*` axis is refused: costs are sensitivities inside a trial (backtest spec req 6), the universe is not tuned on P&L (ADR 0006 guard a), and the rest are data or accounting conventions), and `[lab]` (`selection_statistic`; `expected_excess_cagr_spy_pp` and `expected_range_pp = [lo, hi]`; `promote_at_least`, a `dsr_excess` floor ≥ `lab.promotion_min_dsr_excess`; `retire_below`, a floor on the selection statistic). The Cartesian product is expanded, refused above `lab.max_variants_per_sweep`, and each variant's frozen set is built exactly as `backtest/hypothesis.py` builds a hypothesis's (file values over live `Settings`, validated through `Settings`, hashed). The command stores the sweep (`sweeps` row: file hash, grid JSON and hash, declared variant count, the `[lab]` values) and registers each variant through `registry.register_hypothesis` with the sweep file as `doc_path`, the variant slug, the sweep's family, window and holdout, and one `sweep_variants` row. Registration is idempotent for an unchanged file and frozen sets; a changed file is a new sweep and new variants (the Phase 3 rule). **Holdout coverage:** a sweep whose `[holdout]` does not cover the family holdout (`start` ≤ the family's minimum start, `end` ≥ its maximum end) is refused; so no lab trial's in-sample window can overlap any family member's holdout (req 5). **No back door:** `hypothesis register` refuses a standalone file whose `params_sha256` equals any `sweep_variants` member's in the same family unless an `owner_decisions` row of kind `promotion` names that variant and that file (req 4), so a winning variant cannot be re-registered with a clean story.
2. **Sweep run: every variant a trial, one read set per group, no flags.** `tradepartner sweep run <sweep-slug> [--time-budget-minutes N] [--note]` runs, in canonical order, every variant that has no `ok`, non-synthetic, in-sample trial over the sweep window under the current registration. The window is always the variant's default in-sample window (`holdout.default_in_sample_window`); the command has no `--start`, `--end`, `--spend-holdout`, `--holdout-repeat`, `--override-gap` or store-path option (parser test), so a sweep cannot reach the holdout or the gap override at all. Variants are grouped into read groups; `engine.run_many` runs a group from **one read set per step** (the marking and signal frames read for the union of the group's ids; each variant restricted to its own) and returns per-variant results identical to separate `engine.run` calls. Every variant of a group has its own `TrialHandle` from `registry.open_trial` **before** the group's first provider call, and `run_many` raises before any read if any variant lacks one (no id, no run; ADR 0005). A failure in one variant closes that variant `failed` and does not fail the others; the `store changed during run` rule applies to the group (every open variant of the group fails with that message). Each run appends a `sweep_runs` row (declared, planned, `ok`, failed counts, seconds, code version, `run_by`) and one `sweep_trials` row per trial. The run stops cleanly when the time budget (`--time-budget-minutes`, default `lab.sweep_time_budget_minutes`) is exhausted at a group boundary; the sweep is then **incomplete** and the next `sweep run` resumes with the remaining variants. A rerun of a complete sweep (`--rerun`) runs every variant again: every rerun is a new trial, N rises by the variant count, V takes the latest per pair (backtest spec req 8). Trials are written at the sweep's detail level (req 14). Sensitivities per cost level are computed for every variant as in Phase 3 and are never new trials.
3. **Sweep accounting and report.** N and V are the family's, unchanged: N counts every `ok`, non-synthetic, in-sample trial of the family including every variant's; V is over the latest `ok` trial per (parameter hash, window) pair (req 9 fixes the unit). A sweep has no N of its own. `tradepartner sweep report <sweep-slug>` prints, for the sweep's current registration: the declared and run variant counts and whether the sweep is complete; the family's N, V and SR* today, and SR* recomputed at the **declared** variant count as a lower bound ("SR* at declared count"); per variant, one row with its slug, the varied values, the base-level `excess_cagr_spy`, `sharpe_annual_excess_spy`, `dsr_excess` (stored, and recomputed with today's N and V), `cost_drag`, `turnover_annual`, `max_drawdown`, `red_flag` and status, in canonical order; the distribution of the selection statistic (minimum, quartiles, median, maximum, the share of variants with `dsr_excess` > 0.5 and the share inside `expected_range_pp`); and, **only for a complete sweep**, the argmax variant and whether `promote_at_least` and `retire_below` are met. An incomplete sweep's report prints no argmax and no verdict line. A variant whose base-level `excess_cagr_spy` exceeds `metrics.red_flag_excess_cagr_pp` is `red_flag` like any trial (backtest spec req 15); the report counts them. The report never prints a holdout value, because no variant has one.
4. **Promotion, capped and pre-declared.** `tradepartner sweep promote <sweep-slug> --file docs/hypotheses/<slug>.md --reason` is refused unless: the sweep is complete; fewer than `lab.max_promotions_per_sweep` promotions exist for it; the file's frozen parameters (`params_sha256`) equal those of the variant that maximises the sweep's selection statistic at the base cost level (ties by canonical index; a non-argmax file is refused, however good the reason: a different rule is a new sweep); that variant's `dsr_excess`, recomputed with today's N and V, is ≥ the sweep's `promote_at_least`; and the file carries a `## Sweep provenance` section naming the sweep slug and registration, the declared variant count, the variant slug and rank, and its base-level in-sample statistics, plus the usual hypothesis sections (its prior-evidence disclosure lists the sweep's results as evidence already seen). The command registers the file (a standalone hypothesis in the same family), appends an `owner_decisions` row of kind `promotion` (values: sweep id, variant hypothesis id, promoted hypothesis id, declared count, family N and the statistic at promotion) and prints both ids. The promoted hypothesis then follows the Phase 3 rules unchanged: `backtest <slug>` runs it (each run a trial), and only the owner may spend its holdout, once, with `--spend-holdout --holdout-reason`, marked `holdout_repeat` when any hypothesis in the family already spent it (backtest spec req 11). The page and the report show "promoted from <sweep>, 1 of <n>" beside it. `sweep retire <sweep-slug> --reason` appends an `owner_decisions` row of kind `sweep_retired`; the report shows it. Neither promotion nor retirement changes N.
5. **Holdout rules restated for volume.** (a) A sweep variant is refused by `backtest <slug>` (`refused_variant`, exit 2, a trial with that status like any refusal, no provider call): variants run only through `sweep run`, which cannot spend the holdout (req 2). (b) A holdout spend is one per hypothesis by the owner, exactly as in Phase 3; the lab adds no spend path and removes none. (c) The family holdout only grows: `hypothesis register` and `sweep register` refuse a holdout that does not cover the family holdout at registration time, so a later hypothesis cannot move its in-sample window over an earlier one's holdout. (d) `lab replay` refuses a window that touches the hypothesis's frozen holdout or ends after it, with no flag to pass (req 12). (e) A `tracking` trial (Phase 4) is unchanged and never counts.
6. **Cadence as a frozen parameter.** A new frozen section `schedule` (`FROZEN_SECTIONS` gains it; `backtest/hypothesis.py` freezes it like the others) holds `schedule.rebalance_cadence` (default `month_end`) and `schedule.signal_anchor` (default `month_end`). A hypothesis file may pin both; a sweep file must name `schedule.rebalance_cadence`. `schedule.rebalance_sessions(start, end, cadence)`, `fill_session`, `read_time` and `holdout.gap_sessions(window, cadence)` take the cadence; the engine, the results writer, the holdout decision (its "touches the holdout but reaches none of its rebalance sessions" rule and the gap gate's sessions) and the registry's window key (`_first_rebalance_on_or_after`) read it from the frozen parameters. `calendar.last_session_of_week(iso_year, iso_week)` and `calendar.rebalance_sessions_between(start, end, cadence)` come from `all_sessions()`. At `week_end` the rebalance is the last session of the ISO week, so a Good Friday week rebalances on Thursday; at `daily` every session is a rebalance and F_i is the next session. Forced exits, `stale_exit_sessions`, `missing_fill`, late dividends, the marking frame and one-frame-per-step valuation are per step exactly as in Phase 3, with "month" read as "period". **Phase 4 stays monthly** (req 11).
7. **Signal anchor.** `signals.momentum(frame, t_session, formation_months, skip_months, anchor, cadence)` scores a name as close(A_skip)/close(A_form) − 1 where, under `anchor = month_end`, A_k is the last XNYS session of the calendar month k months before T's month (the Phase 3 rule; at `month_end` cadence identical to `momentum_12_1`), and under `anchor = offset`, A_k is the last session on or before the calendar date T − k months (`dateutil.relativedelta`). Under `month_end` the score changes only when a month ends, so a weekly or daily rebalance re-trades drift and universe changes between month ends; under `offset` it refreshes every rebalance. Both read only sessions ≤ T. A name lacking either bar is excluded and counted, as before. Cadence and anchor are two named keys so a sweep can vary them separately; the combination is stated in every report row.
8. **Metrics annualised from the schedule.** `metrics.series_metrics(period_returns, periods_per_year, ...)` computes, per series and cost level, the Phase 3 keys with the period keys renamed and three added: `cagr` (years = `n_periods` / `periods_per_year`), `vol_annual` (period std × √ppy), `sharpe_period` (non-annualised, `metrics.risk_free_rate` converted to the period), `sharpe_annual` (= `sharpe_period` × √ppy), `sharpe_period_excess_spy`, `sharpe_annual_excess_spy` (new), `max_drawdown` (daily equity, cadence-independent), `turnover_period` (one-sided average per rebalance), `turnover_annual` (new; = `turnover_period` × ppy), `cost_drag`, `excess_cagr_spy`, `excess_cagr_mtum`, `tracking_error_spy`, `tracking_error_mtum` (× √ppy), `skew_period`, `kurtosis_period`, `skew_period_excess_spy`, `kurtosis_period_excess_spy`, `n_periods`, `periods_per_year` (new; the constant, stored so a reader never guesses). The `*_monthly` and `n_months` keys are no longer written. The additive migration (the next free schema version) inserts, for every existing trial, the `*_period` rows copied from its `*_monthly` rows, `n_periods` from `n_months`, `periods_per_year = 12`, `turnover_annual = 12 × turnover_monthly` and `sharpe_annual_excess_spy = √12 × sharpe_monthly_excess_spy`; the old rows stay (append-only) and nothing reads them afterwards. The cost model is unchanged at every cadence: cost per trade = notional × `costs.per_side_bps`/10⁴ + commissions, on every fill including exits; the ladder applies; what changes with cadence is how often trades happen, which `turnover_annual` and `cost_drag` show. The AST literal check on `backtest/{metrics,costs,fills,valuation,engine,schedule}.py` allows 0, 1, −1, 2, 4, 10 000 and the three `PERIODS_PER_YEAR` entries in `schedule.py` only.
9. **Deflated Sharpe across cadences.** The DSR is computed at each trial's own period: SR = `sharpe_period` (or `sharpe_period_excess_spy`), T = `n_periods`, γ₃ and γ₄ the period skew and kurtosis. **V is the sample variance of annualised Sharpes**: `family_sharpes` reads `sharpe_period`, `sharpe_period_excess_spy` and `periods_per_year` and returns each counted trial's Sharpe × √ppy per basis; SR*_annual = √V · ((1−γ)Φ⁻¹(1−1/N) + γΦ⁻¹(1−1/(N·e))) as before, and the trial's SR* = SR*_annual / √(its ppy). For a family whose trials are all `month_end` this equals the Phase 3 computation exactly (V_annual = 12·V_monthly, so SR*_annual/√12 = SR*_monthly), and the Phase 3 reference values hold. The page and the report recompute DSR with today's N and V as before. This is the one arithmetic change to backtest spec req 8 (amendment 5 below). The formula assumes Sharpe scales with √k under aggregation and ignores period autocorrelation; the risk section says so.
10. **Engine, checks and oracle per cadence.** `engine.run` and `run_many` iterate `rebalance_sessions(start, end, cadence)`; `results.write_results` derives period ends from the same function. The Phase 3 look-ahead suites (truncation invariance, prefix invariance, the revision case, the plan-read timing check) and the `bt` oracle are parametrised over the three cadences on the fixture store; `run_many` on a group passes truncation invariance per variant. The gap gate reads `survivorship_gap` at every rebalance close of a holdout window at the hypothesis's cadence (never a sweep's: sweeps have no holdout runs).
11. **Phase 4 stays monthly; ADR 0005 unchanged.** `paper start --hypothesis` refuses (`refused_cadence`) a hypothesis whose frozen `schedule.rebalance_cadence` is not `month_end`; the paper spec's T_i, `paper.min_rebalances = 6` and the monthly tracking comparison are as written. Paper trading at another cadence is a Phase 4 spec amendment made when, and only when, a non-monthly hypothesis is promoted toward paper (out of scope here). ADR 0005's six-rebalance criterion, its integrity stop criterion ("any untracked backtest run halts the phase": the lab keeps every run registered) and its budget line are untouched; no replay row is evidence for any of them.
12. **Shadow-paper replay: a test and calibration harness, never evidence.** `tradepartner lab replay <slug> --start <session> --end <session> [--fill-model <name>] [--fault-script <file>] [--seed N] [--keep-copy]` (owner on the real store; agents and tests with `store_path` set to a fixture): (a) copies the store file to `<replay.dir>/<slug>-<replay_id>.duckdb` and refuses to proceed if the copy's path equals `settings.store.path`; every trial the replay opens is on the copy and therefore `synthetic` and outside N, V and holdout spends; (b) refuses a window not inside the hypothesis's in-sample window (`refused_window` before any read; no flag exists), so the replay can never show holdout or post-holdout returns; (c) runs `execution.run.tracking_run` once per session of the window against a `ScriptedBroker` (a `FakeBroker` subclass with `account`, `open_orders`, `get_order`, `fills(since)`, `assets`) and a `ReplayClock` that visits only three instants per session, pre-open (open(S) − `paper.submit_window_before_open_minutes` + 1 minute), open(S) + `paper.sell_wait_seconds` and close(S), so no intraday data is read or implied; the paper window row, the `risk.*` freeze, the two-phase wrapper, the journal chain, reconciliation, marks, the halt path and the kill switch run as the paper spec and ADR 0010 say, with `alerts.channels` forced to `[store]` on the copy; (d) fills each order at the fill model's reference bar price of the order's session (`replay.fill_reference` ∈ {`open`, `close`}) × (1 + side × slippage), slippage drawn once per order from N(`replay.slippage_bps_mean`, `replay.slippage_bps_std`) with `replay.seed` (a replay is deterministic given its seed and script), partially filled, expired or rejected with `replay.partial_fill_probability`, `replay.expiry_probability`, `replay.rejection_probability`, each draw journaled by the scripted broker with its reason; the fault script's events override the draws on their sessions; (e) after a halt, the replay issues its scripted `paper resume --reason "replay script"` on the session the script names (default: the next session), so the run continues and every halt, resume and reconciliation status is counted; (f) never imports or constructs `AlpacaBroker` (AST check on `execution/replay.py` and `lab/`), never reads `ALPACA_*` variables, and never writes to `settings.store.path` except one `lab_replays` summary row (req 13).
13. **Replay outputs.** Per replay, in the copy: every journal row the tracking run wrote (the same tables as paper). In the real store, one `lab_replays` row: hypothesis id, window, fill model name and its values, seed, fault-script hash, `calibrated_from_window_id` (null while the fill model is a placeholder), per-order fill-timing distribution quantiles (paper fill price minus the frozen `execution.fill_price` bar price, signed as the paper spec's fill-timing term, over equity: p5, p25, p50, p75, p95, mean), the per-rebalance modelled-cost and realised-cost distributions, the count of halts by fault type, resumes, reconciliation statuses, missed and executed rebalances, `unspent_cash` alerts, and the report path. `tradepartner lab report <replay_id>` prints them and, for a `month_end` hypothesis, the share of months whose replayed fill-timing term alone exceeds `paper.tracking_k` × the modelled cost (the tracking tolerance the paper phase is judged by), which is the calibration readout: it says whether the fill model would breach the check, not whether paper will. The fill-model keys are placeholders until the first paper window closes; the owner then sets them from the paper journal's fill-timing term (`lab calibrate --window <id>` prints the fitted values and the config lines; setting them is a config change) and every later replay row carries that `calibrated_from_window_id`. The trials page shows replays under a heading that reads "Replay (simulated, not evidence)"; `paper report`, `paper check` and `family_sharpes` never read `lab_replays` (text check).
14. **Storage per trial.** `trials` gains a `detail_level` column (additive migration; `full` for every existing row). Standalone hypotheses store `full` (the Phase 3 tables as written). Sweep variants store `lab.sweep_detail_level` (default `summary`): `trial_metrics` per series and level, `trial_results`, `trial_rebalances` per level, `trial_equity` daily for every series at the base level and at rebalance sessions only for the other levels, and no `trial_weights`. Metrics, including `max_drawdown` per level, are computed from the full in-memory result before the rows are chosen, so a summary trial's metrics equal a full trial's. Anything not stored is recomputed by a rerun, which is a new trial. The page shows a summary trial's base-level equity with the detail level stated and no weights table.
15. **Compute and registry growth.** Row counts per trial are stated so the budget is arithmetic, not hope: at `full` and `month_end` over H1's window, about 3 series × 4 levels × 1,750 sessions ≈ 21,000 equity rows plus about 100 names × 84 rebalances ≈ 8,400 weight rows; at `summary`, about 3 × 1,750 + 3 × 3 × 84 ≈ 6,000 equity rows and no weights; at `daily` cadence, `trial_rebalances` grows to about 1,750 rows per level and `full` weights to about 175,000 rows, which is why sweeps default to `summary`. `tradepartner lab status` prints the store file size, the row count of every registry table, the family N and V, open sweeps with their completion, and the last ten `sweep_runs` with seconds per variant. `sweep run` refuses to start when the store file exceeds `lab.registry_size_refuse_gb` and warns above `lab.registry_size_warn_gb`. Per-step cost at `daily` cadence is a universe rebuild and a gap read per session, about 21 times the monthly count; the plan's first task measures seconds per step per cadence on the owner's store (owner task) and records it in the plan's verification section, and `sweep_runs` records seconds per variant from then on, so the time budget is set from measurements. Read groups are the only sharing: one process, no worker pool in this spec (open question 8).
16. **Family and sweep view on the trials page.** `dashboard/trials_page.py` gains, above the trial table, a **families** card per family (N today, V today per basis in annual units, SR*_annual, holdout spends with the spending hypothesis, promotions) and a **sweeps** table (slug, registration, declared and run counts, complete or not, the selection-statistic quartiles, the `dsr_excess` > 0.5 share, red-flag count, argmax only when complete, promotion and retirement rows), and a **replays** table under the "Replay (simulated, not evidence)" heading. Read-only, inside the shell's single read-only connection, per the design standard; no run button.
17. **CLI.** `tradepartner sweep register <file> | run <slug> [--time-budget-minutes] [--rerun] [--note] | status [<slug>] | report <slug> | promote <slug> --file <path> --reason | retire <slug> --reason`; `tradepartner lab replay <slug> --start --end [--fill-model] [--fault-script] [--seed] [--keep-copy] | report <replay_id> | calibrate --window <id> | status`. `backtest <variant-slug>` exits 2 `refused_variant`. No command takes a store path, a holdout, gap or synthetic flag (parser test); output contains no secret-shaped string.

## Acceptance criteria (testable)

Sweep registration and guardrails
- [ ] `sweep register` on the fixture sweep file (two axes, 2 × 2 = 4 variants) writes one `sweeps` row and four `hypotheses` rows with slugs `<slug>--v001` to `--v004` in ascending `params_sha256`, four `sweep_variants` rows, the sweep's family, window and holdout on every variant, and `doc_path` = the sweep file; re-running it writes nothing; a changed grid writes a new sweep and new variants; the grid axes are in `lab.sweepable_keys`; a `costs.per_side_bps`, `universe.top_n_by_cap` or `holdout.start` axis is refused; a key both fixed and gridded is refused; a product above `lab.max_variants_per_sweep` (set to 3 in the test) is refused with no row written; a file missing any `strategy.*` or `costs.*` key or `schedule.rebalance_cadence` is refused; a `[lab]` block missing `selection_statistic`, `expected_range_pp`, `promote_at_least` or `retire_below`, or with `promote_at_least` below `lab.promotion_min_dsr_excess`, is refused.
- [ ] Holdout coverage: with a registered hypothesis whose holdout is [2024-01-01, 2026-09-30], a sweep or standalone file with `holdout.start = 2025-01-01` or `holdout.end = 2026-06-30` is refused naming the family holdout; one with [2023-06-30, 2026-12-31] is accepted and the family holdout becomes that.
- [ ] No back door: `hypothesis register` on a standalone file whose frozen set equals variant `--v002`'s is refused naming the variant; after `sweep promote` names that variant and that file, the same command (as `promote` runs it) succeeds.
- [ ] `backtest <variant-slug>` exits 2, leaves a `trials` and `trial_results` row with status `refused_variant`, makes no provider call and leaves N and V unchanged; the `sweep run` and `lab replay` parsers have no `--start`/`--end` (sweep), `--spend-holdout`, `--holdout-repeat`, `--override-gap`, `--synthetic` or store-path option.

Sweep run and accounting
- [ ] `sweep run` on the fixture store runs the four variants in canonical order, each with its own `TrialHandle` opened before the group's first provider call (recording provider, recording registry), writes four `ok` trials over the default in-sample window, one `sweep_runs` row and four `sweep_trials` rows; N rises by four; `family_sharpes` returns four annualised Sharpes; a second `sweep run` without `--rerun` runs nothing; with `--rerun` it runs four more trials, N rises by four and V is over four pairs.
- [ ] Read groups: the fixture's two variants that differ only in `strategy.top_fraction` make exactly the provider calls of one `engine.run` per step (recording provider, call count equality), and their results equal separate `engine.run` results row for row (equity, rebalances, metrics); two variants that differ in `schedule.rebalance_cadence` or `strategy.signal_total_return` are separate groups; `run_many` with a variant lacking a handle raises `TypeError` before any provider call.
- [ ] Failure isolation: an injected provider error on variant 3 leaves variants 1, 2 and 4 `ok` and variant 3 `failed`; a row inserted mid-group fails every open variant of that group with `store changed during run` and the next `sweep run` resumes them; a time budget of one group (fake clock) stops after the first group with `sweep_runs.n_ok` equal to that group's size and the sweep incomplete.
- [ ] `sweep report` on an incomplete sweep prints the counts, the per-variant rows for the trials that exist, the family N and "SR* at declared count", and neither an argmax nor a verdict line; on the complete sweep it prints the argmax at the base cost level with ties broken by canonical index, the quartiles, the `dsr_excess` > 0.5 share, the share inside `expected_range_pp`, the red-flag count (one variant given an `excess_cagr_spy` above the threshold by a synthetic frame) and the `promote_at_least`/`retire_below` verdicts; no holdout value appears in the output (text check).

Promotion
- [ ] `sweep promote` is refused while the sweep is incomplete; with a file whose parameters are not the argmax variant's; with a file missing `## Sweep provenance`; when the argmax's recomputed `dsr_excess` is below `promote_at_least`; and for a second promotion when `lab.max_promotions_per_sweep = 1`. With the argmax file it registers the hypothesis, appends the `promotion` decision with the declared count, family N and statistic, and `backtest <promoted-slug>` runs as an ordinary in-sample trial; `--spend-holdout --holdout-reason` on it runs as `kind=holdout`, marked `holdout_repeat` when another hypothesis of the family has spent, and a second spend is refused without `--holdout-repeat` (the Phase 3 suite, run on the promoted slug); N is unchanged by the promotion itself. `sweep retire --reason` appends `sweep_retired` and changes N by nothing.

## Risks & domain checks

- **Look-ahead:** every cadence reads at close(T_i) through the same `t`-parameterised provider; the anchor rules read sessions ≤ T only; the Phase 3 suites (truncation, prefix, revision, plan-read timing) run at every cadence and per variant of a read group; a read group's union read is a superset of each variant's, restricted per variant, so no variant sees another's holdings; the replay plans through the engine's own function under a plan trial at close(T_i) and fills from the fill session's bar, the backtest convention (a fill is never a plan input).
- **Multiple testing:** every variant is a hypothesis and every run a trial (no handle, no read, per variant); N counts variants, reruns and failures-turned-ok alike; a sweep's declared count is shown as the SR* lower bound before it completes; the canonical order and the no-window rule stop stopping-early and window-fishing; `backtest` refuses variants; a variant's parameters cannot be re-registered without a promotion; promotion is argmax of a pre-declared statistic with a pre-declared floor, capped per sweep, logged with N; the report shows the whole distribution beside the argmax; the family holdout only grows, so no later sweep can turn an earlier holdout into in-sample; sensitivities never select; synthetic and oracle trials stay impossible on the real store; agents run only on fixtures.
- **Holdout:** no sweep path reaches it (parser and decision tests); the replay refuses it; the promoted hypothesis spends it under the Phase 3 rules only; the sweep report and the page print no holdout value for variants.
- **Costs:** per trade, cadence-independent; `turnover_annual` and `cost_drag` show what a faster cadence costs; the ladder applies to every variant; no market impact is modelled at any cadence, so `daily` results at the base level are optimistic, which the ladder bounds. The replay's slippage is a placeholder model until fitted to paper fills, and the row says so (`calibrated_from_window_id`).
- **Statistics at faster cadences:** the DSR formula and the √ppy conversion assume Sharpe scales with √k under aggregation and that period returns are not autocorrelated; at `daily` cadence T is large and autocorrelation is not zero, so a daily DSR is more confident than a monthly one for the same annual Sharpe by construction; open question 4 keeps the constants and records the approximation; the annual-unit V makes cadences comparable but not identical in power.
- **Survivorship:** the gap is recorded per rebalance at every cadence and gated on holdout runs as before; at `daily` the gap read per session is the compute cost, not a bias.
- **Compute and the owner's time:** thousands of runs are hours on the owner's machine; the time budget, read groups, `summary` detail and the size guards bound it; the measurement task turns the placeholders into numbers; `sweep run` outside the ingest window avoids `store changed during run` (the runbook says so).
- **Registry migration:** renaming the period keys is done by inserting rows, never editing; a Phase 3 trial's DSR is unchanged (fixture comparison); adding `schedule` to the frozen set makes an older registration unrunnable until re-registered (`hypothesis.load_frozen`'s rule), so H1, if registered before this ships, is re-registered once by the owner (open question 2).
- **Phase 4 coupling:** `paper start` refuses non-monthly cadences, so the six-rebalance criterion stays monthly and ADR 0005 needs no change; the replay reuses the tracking run unchanged on a copy with a fake, so it exercises the real order path without an endpoint; `alerts.channels` forced to `[store]` on the copy keeps replays silent.
- **Order path, secrets, LLM inputs:** the replay constructs `ScriptedBroker` only (AST), reads no `ALPACA_*` variable and no paper key, and nothing here emits or consumes model output. `safety-reviewer` runs on the replay PRs (they touch `execution/`); `quant-auditor` on everything else.
- **Terms and budget:** no new data, API or spend (ADR 0009's $0 budget holds); the store copy is a private local file under Alpaca's personal-use terms.

## Open questions

Answers go on #281. Questions 1 to 3 need an answer before the plan; the rest before the task that implements them.

1. **Terms.** The issue says "family of hypotheses from one file". Options: (a) keep `family` as the DSR unit (Phase 3) and call the file's grid a **sweep** (spec'd); (b) rename the Phase 3 family to "signal family" and use "family" for the grid. Recommendation: (a): `hypotheses.families`, `family_sharpes` and `family_holdout_spends` already exist and the DSR guarantee is stated in those words.
2. **Where the cadence key lives and what it does to H1.** Options: (a) a new frozen section `schedule` with defaults, not required in a hypothesis file, so H1's file is unchanged and its registration is re-run once by the owner after the key lands (a new `hypotheses` row, same slug and file; the older row's trials stay in N; a superseded same-slug, same-file registration is folded into its successor's V pair by `family_sharpes`, so V is not diluted by a duplicate); (b) put the key under `strategy.*`, which every file must name, so H1's file is edited before registration (a new hypothesis if it was already registered). Recommendation: (a), with the plan ordering the key before T45b where possible.
3. **Signal anchor.** Options: (a) `schedule.signal_anchor ∈ {month_end, offset}`, default `month_end` (spec'd); (b) `offset` at every non-monthly cadence with no key. Recommendation: (a): two keys make the confound visible and sweepable.
4. **Periods per year.** Options: (a) constants 12/52/252 (spec'd); (b) computed from the calendar over the run (sessions per calendar year, ISO weeks per year); (c) constants for Sharpe and volatility, calendar years for `cagr`. Recommendation: (a): H1's stored `cagr` is unchanged and the daily exponent error is under one percent; record (c) as a retro item.
5. **Promotion floor.** `lab.promotion_min_dsr_excess = 0.5` and a sweep's `promote_at_least` at or above it. Options: (a) 0.5 (the argmax of N null trials has DSR ≈ 0.5, so the floor means "better than the luckiest null trial"); (b) 0.95 (Bailey and López de Prado's conventional confidence; with H1's power arithmetic almost nothing plausible passes, so promotion becomes theatre in the other direction). Recommendation: (a) as the floor, with the sweep free to pre-register higher.
6. **Sweep size cap** `lab.max_variants_per_sweep = 100`. Options: (a) 100; (b) 1,000 with the time budget as the only brake. Recommendation: (a): at G4's "best 3 of 20 equals best of 1,250", a hundred variants is already a lot of selection, and a larger question is two sweeps.
7. **Registry size guards** (20 GB warn, 50 GB refuse) and `summary` as the sweep default. Recommendation: accept as placeholders and set from the measurement task's bytes per trial.
8. **Parallelism.** Options: (a) one process, read groups only (spec'd); (b) a worker pool of read-only processes with per-variant write chunks. Recommendation: (a) now; (b) is a follow-up issue once seconds per step are measured.
9. **Replay fill-model placeholders** (`replay.slippage_bps_mean = 5`, `std = 10`, the three probabilities). Recommendation: accept as placeholders, mark every replay row `calibrated_from_window_id = null` until the first paper window closes, then set them with `lab calibrate`.
10. **Replay reference bar** `replay.fill_reference = open`. Options: (a) `open`, so the timing term against H1's frozen `close` is non-zero and visible; (b) the hypothesis's own `execution.fill_price`, so the term is slippage only. Recommendation: (a); (b) is one config change when wanted.
11. **Metric key rename.** Options: (a) rename `*_monthly` → `*_period` with the row-inserting migration (spec'd); (b) keep the old names and add `periods_per_year`. Recommendation: (a): a `sharpe_monthly` on a daily trial would be a lie in a column name.
12. **Standalone hypotheses and the growing family holdout** (req 5c). Options: (a) hard refusal for sweeps and standalone files alike (spec'd); (b) hard for sweeps, an owner flag with reason for standalone files. Recommendation: (a): a flag is a path to the leak this rule closes.
