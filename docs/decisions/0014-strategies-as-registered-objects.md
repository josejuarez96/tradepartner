# 0014. Strategies are registered objects: one dispatch seam, one family registry, generic exclusions

**Status:** Accepted (owner decisions of 2026-10-06, in chat to the orchestrator, recorded under "Owner decisions" below; by merging PR #1077, a class-B merge by the owner)  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-06  ·  **Issue:** #1074  ·  **Plan:** [strategy-interface](../plans/strategy-interface.md)  ·  **Related:** [backtest spec](../specs/backtest.md) (amendment #720, the `profitability` family), [backtest plan](../plans/backtest.md) (T85 to T85f), [strategy-lab spec](../specs/strategy-lab.md), [ADR 0005](0005-objective-benchmark-stop-criteria.md) (benchmarks), [ADR 0012](0012-cadence-as-a-hypothesis-parameter.md) (cadence)

## Context

In plain words: the code has no idea called "a strategy". It has a family name, `momentum`, that a dozen places each read for themselves. The engine always calls the momentum signal; the paper journal only knows momentum's one reason for leaving a stock out ("no history"); the trial registry has one count column shaped for momentum; every result must be compared with MTUM, a momentum ETF; and a family that is missing from any of the small tables that list families quietly inherits momentum's settings instead of failing. Adding the second family, B3 gross profitability, therefore costs six plan tasks (T85 to T85f), and the third would cost about the same again.

Two pieces of evidence sit behind this record, both from 2026-10-06 (owner's probes directory, `tradepartner-probes/strategy-interface/`; summarised on #1074). A **design memo** read five open-source frameworks and mapped where family-specific logic lives on `main`. A **planner spike** then pushed a trivial second strategy (equal weight over every member) through the real paths on the fixture store: `engine.run`, `write_results` and the trial registry, `run_hypothesis` end to end, the T40 truncation and prefix invariance suites, and the paper step `plan_rebalance` → `decisions_from` → the `signals`, `decisions` and `paper_plans` journal rows. Thirteen tests passed on a 22-line seam in three files plus nine lines of family tables; the engine's step, fills, valuation, exits, benchmarks, results, registry and holdout never noticed. Where the memo and the spike disagree, the spike wins. What it found, at `d08d116` (line numbers move; the plan re-verifies them per task):

1. **Nothing picks the strategy from the hypothesis.** `engine.run` calls the private `_plan` (`backtest/engine.py:170-193`, called at `:490` and `:520`), which calls `momentum_12_1` outright. `run_hypothesis` (`backtest/run.py:148`) never reads `hypothesis.family` to choose a signal, so its callers, the CLI (`cli.py:823`), the paper run (`execution/run.py:1824` → `execution/planning.py:536` `_plan_at` → `engine.plan`) and the paper report's tracking re-run (`execution/report.py:891`), would all run momentum for any slug. Passing a planner in from a caller, as the spike did, is not enough: the lookup has to happen inside `run_hypothesis` and `plan_rebalance`, keyed on the stored family.
2. **One exclusion reason, in three places.** `Plan` has `n_excluded_no_history` and `excluded_no_history` (`engine.py:97-115`); `trial_rebalances.n_excluded_no_history` is `NOT NULL` (`store/schema.py:844`, `store/registry.py:240`); the paper journal's `_signals` asserts `scores ∪ excluded_no_history == members` (`execution/plan.py:927-934`) and the `signals.reason` CHECK allows exactly `selected`, `below_cut`, `excluded_no_history` (`schema.py:1003`). A strategy with any other exclusion reason is refused by the paper path with a raw `ValueError`. This is the one real schema boundary; T85d's six fixed profitability columns deepen it.
3. **Silent momentum fallbacks.** `frozen.inert_sections` falls back to momentum's section for an unlisted family (`backtest/frozen.py:157`); `hypothesis.required_keys` does the same (`backtest/hypothesis.py:134`), and `frozen_params_of` and `check_frozen` default `family="momentum"` (`:239`, `:251`). The spike's family froze, hashed and required `strategy.*` keys it never read, so its `params_sha256` changed with `strategy.top_fraction`.
4. **The family list lives in four places** (`config.py:618-645`: the `Literal`, `_HYPOTHESIS_FAMILIES`, `FAMILY_PARENTS`, `ENGINE_FAMILIES`) plus `FAMILY_SIGNAL_SECTIONS` (`frozen.py:102`), `REQUIRED_SECTIONS` (`hypothesis.py:78`) and the sweep prefixes (`config.py:871-873`), and the `ENGINE_FAMILIES` gate is repeated at `run.py:190`, `planning.py:451` and `window.py:590`.
5. **MTUM is mandatory for every family**: `metrics.Series` and the metric keys `excess_cagr_mtum`, `tracking_error_mtum` (`backtest/metrics.py:41-56`), and `results._check` refuses a trial without MTUM equity (`backtest/results.py:66-67`, `:84`).
6. **A latent cadence bug, not of this record's making:** the frozen `schedule.rebalance_cadence` and `schedule.signal_anchor` are hashed but read nowhere; a `week_end` registration ran eight month-end rebalances and wrote `ok`. The strategy-lab plan's T98 (engine at cadence) and T100 (`paper start` refuses non-monthly) cover it; this record only sequences its tasks after T98 where they share `engine.run`.

What the spike confirmed is already generic and must stay so: the `DataProvider` protocol with every read as-of `t`, the engine step, fills, valuation, costs and exits, `write_results`, the registry's trial handle and family N, `holdout.decide`, the risk rules over the frozen `risk.*` section, and the shape of the look-ahead suites.

## Options considered

1. **Keep the per-family tables and add each family by hand** (B3's path). Pro: nothing to refactor; the tables are explicit. Con: six tasks and four shared files per family, `engine.py`, `schema.py`, `execution/plan.py` and the CHECK enum edited every time, and the fallbacks stay silent. The memo priced strategy #3 at about a working week of agent time across teams.
2. **A full strategy object model** (the memo's `Strategy` protocol with reader, scorer and constructor, `AsOfView`, `Scores`, a `StrategySpec` registry, a lane-1 diagnostic). Pro: the cleanest layering, qstrader's stages with hikyuu's one object for backtest and paper. Con: the spike showed the engine core needs no change at all and that the real cost sits in two places (the dispatch and the exclusions/counts shape); a protocol with three abstract parts for three families is abstraction ahead of need, and the memo itself names a third method creeping in as the signal to stop.
3. **This record: one seam, one registry, generic exclusions and counts, per-family benchmarks**, each the smallest change that makes the next family a registry entry plus its own module. Size M, about four tasks. Pro: everything the spike found blocking or silent is fixed; nothing the spike found generic is touched; H1's identity is pinned byte for byte. Con: `Plan` keeps its momentum-named fields as derived values for one more plan, and the paper journal takes one additive migration during Phase 4's paper window (kept small and last).

**Considered, prior art** (the memo's reading; one line each, what we borrow): **qstrader**, pure `(dt, weights) -> weights` stages, each swappable, with the risk stage mandatory (ours already is, in `execution/risk.py`); **hikyuu**, the one strategy object replaying history and then running live, which is why the seam sits in both `run_hypothesis` and `plan_rebalance`; **vectorbt**, a strategy's only output is a weight frame and a separate deterministic simulator turns it into orders, and one variant per column is our "one variant, one counted trial"; **myhhub/stock**, a single self-describing registry list where adding a strategy is appending one entry; **stock-analysis-engine**, the full resolved config saved inside every run artifact, which our trial row already does. None of the five has point-in-time data, delisted names, a counted trial registry or a holdout; those stay ours.

## Decision

We will take option 3. Concretely:

1. **One dispatch seam, keyed on the stored family, inside the engine's two entry points.** `engine.run` and `engine.plan` take the family; `run_hypothesis` passes `hypothesis.family` and the paper planning step passes the window's hypothesis family, so the CLI, the paper run and the paper report's tracking re-run all dispatch without being edited. The lookup is `signal_for(family)` in a leaf module `backtest/strategies.py`, a table keyed by family that returns the reads the signal makes, the pure signal and the counts it reports. An unknown family raises before any provider call. The backtest plan's T85e builds this seam (its line is amended on #1074) so that the codebase ends up with one seam, not two.
2. **One family registry, from which the tables derive.** `config.FAMILIES` maps each `HypothesisFamily` to a frozen spec: its parameter section (the `Settings` field), its parent or `None`, whether the engine and the paper path may run it, its exclusion reasons, its count names, its comparison benchmark or `None`, and its sweepable keys. `FAMILY_PARENTS`, `ENGINE_FAMILIES`, `PAPER_FAMILIES`, `FAMILY_SIGNAL_SECTIONS`, `REQUIRED_SECTIONS`, the family part of `FROZEN_SECTIONS`, `ALLOWED_AXIS_PREFIXES` and the default sweepable keys become derivations with today's values; a test pins that the `HypothesisFamily` literal equals the registry's keys, and another that `strategies.signal_for`'s table has the same keys. The `Literal` stays (mypy strict), the pydantic section per family stays, and `FROZEN_KEY_DEFAULTS` stays append-only: a new family still pays its section's defaults there, which is the lab's integrity rule, not a tax to remove. **The momentum fallbacks go:** an unlisted family fails in `inert_sections`, `fingerprint`, `required_keys`, `frozen_params_of` and `check_frozen` instead of inheriting momentum's identity. `oracle` keeps reading momentum's `strategy` section as an explicit registry entry, so the oracle tests do not move.
3. **Generic exclusions and counts, the one schema change.** `Plan` gains `exclusions` (reason → ids, every reason the family declares present) and `counts` (name → integer); `excluded_no_history` and `n_excluded_no_history` become derived from `exclusions["no_history"]` and stay for the plan-timing suite. The paper journal's partition check becomes "scores and the union of all exclusions partition the members, pairwise disjoint", and the `signals.reason` rule becomes `selected`, `below_cut` or `excluded_<reason>` for a declared reason. The trial registry gains a long-form `trial_rebalance_counts` table (trial, rebalance session, name, value); the fixed count columns (`n_excluded_no_history` and T85d's six) are kept and written from the same mapping by name so both reads agree, their retirement a later size-S issue once the backtest page reads the table. The migration is additive (the count table is new; the `signals` CHECK only widens, so no stored row falls outside it; DuckDB cannot alter a CHECK in place, so `signals` is rebuilt with every row copied, as versions 5 to 8 did for `order_events` and `decisions`).
4. **Per-family benchmarks.** SPY stays mandatory for every family (ADR 0005's objective is return against the benchmark, measured never targeted). The family's comparison benchmark is a registry attribute: `momentum` declares `MTUM` and its metric keys stay `excess_cagr_mtum` and `tracking_error_mtum`, byte for byte; a family may declare another series or none, in which case `results` requires only SPY equity and writes only the SPY keys. The frozen `benchmarks` key and its default `["SPY", "MTUM"]` do not change (it is in H1's fingerprint).
5. **Paper opens per family, by decision.** `PAPER_FAMILIES` is a separate tuple whose value today is `ENGINE_FAMILIES`' value (`momentum`, `oracle`), read by the `paper start` and planning gates. Adding `profitability` to the engine's runnable families therefore does not open paper to it; `paper start` refuses it, naming the family, until point 3 has landed and the owner says so (a one-line registry change, class A by path, labelled `hold`).
6. **Strategy #3 is B4, momentum + profitability combined by fixed equal ranks** ([hypothesis backlog](../research/hypothesis-backlog.md) B4), and it is the proof of this record: it lands as one pure signal module, one registry entry, one `Settings` section with its `.env.example` lines, its `FROZEN_KEY_DEFAULTS` rows, one binding line in `strategies.py`, a fixture twin and its tests, and touches no file under `execution/` or `store/`, nor `engine.py`, `run.py`, `results.py` or `hypothesis.py`. If it needs more, this record was wrong and the plan says where.
7. **A fast "signal quality" lane is a seam, not a build.** `signal_for` returns per-rebalance scores and exclusions that a later diagnostic can join to next-period returns without the portfolio simulation. On the real store such a run touches returns and so is a **research run counted toward the family's N** (research-registry req 9, `research.family_run_count`); the registry rules do not change. Building it is a later task note in the plan, outside this record's scope.

**What does not change:** point-in-time reads (every read as-of `t` through `DataProvider`; the recording fake still records every read), trial counting and the family N, the holdout and `holdout.decide`, the cost model, the deterministic risk gate before any order, the frozen-defaults integrity rule, and H1's identity: `H1_FINGERPRINT` and `TWIN_FINGERPRINT` (`tests/backtest/test_frozen_defaults.py:182-186`) are asserted unchanged by every task of the plan.

**Out of scope** (each needs its own decision or spec): the fast-lane diagnostic's build; long/short or leverage (charter scope; **no signed weights in the type** until an ADR allows them); event-driven reads (B6, B7; the event-data spec) and N-session holds; intraday; hysteresis or holdings-aware construction (a known limit: targets are shared across cost levels, so a holdings-dependent target would need an engine change this record does not make).

## Owner decisions (2026-10-06, in chat to the orchestrator; recorded here as decided)

1. Strategy #3 to prove the interface is B4 (combined), not an event-driven family first (point 6).
2. `paper start` refuses `profitability` until the paper path is generic: a separate paper-families tuple, one line in T85e (point 5).
3. A fast signal-quality lane on the real store counts toward the family's N as a research run, registry rules unchanged; it is out of this plan's build scope, a seam and a later task note only (point 7).
4. No signed weights in the type until an ADR allows long/short (out of scope).
5. The refactor is its own small plan plus this ADR, not a backtest-plan amendment, because it touches the paper path and a schema CHECK.
6. B3 is not paused: T85c, T85d (the six fixed profitability columns), T98 and T77b continue as planned; the refactor lands after or alongside them and migrates T85d's columns (point 3).

## Consequences

- Good: adding a family becomes one module plus one registry entry plus the file-level work that is genuinely new (a read the provider lacks, a hypothesis file); the CLI, the paper run and the tracking re-run agree on which strategy a slug is because there is one lookup; an unlisted family fails instead of becoming momentum; the paper journal can record any declared exclusion reason; a family is compared with the benchmark that makes sense for it; H1's registration and fingerprint are untouched.
- Bad / accepted risks: one additive migration on the paper journal's `signals` table during Phase 4 (kept last and small, `safety-reviewer`); two writes of the same count for one plan (fixed columns and the table) until the columns retire; `Plan` carries derived momentum-named fields a little longer; `engine.py`, `config.py`, `schema.py` and `results.py` are already named by open lab and B3 tasks, so the plan's lanes serialise the refactor behind T98, T85e and T97 and state the disjoint parts.
- Reversibility: cheap for points 1, 2, 4 and 5 (code tables and signatures; every pin test stays); costly for point 3 only in the sense that a schema version cannot be un-shipped, and it is additive.
- Revisit if: a third family needs state the seam cannot pass (holdings, hysteresis) or a hold longer than one period; a reviewer finds a third abstract method creeping into the strategy shape before B4 exists; the owner wants long/short (an ADR) or an event-driven family (the event-data spec's read seam first).
