# 0025. Reconcile by breaks: restrict the name and let the owner true up, instead of halting the whole book

**Status:** Proposed (the owner agreed the direction on #2266, 2026-10-26; accepted by the owner's merge of its PR)  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-26  ·  **Issue:** #2266  ·  **Supersedes, in part:** the [paper spec](../specs/paper-trading.md)'s req 6 sentence "Any residual is `ReconciliationError`: halt, kill switch, alert", for the name-level kinds listed below only; [ADR 0023](0023-break-first-fast-books-paper-exam.md) D2's "Halts stay for a reconciliation mismatch", for the same kinds; [ADR 0010](0010-phase-4-risk-rules.md)'s 2026-10-19 sentence "A short position the journal cannot explain … is a reconciliation mismatch, `ReconciliationError`, never adopted", as far as it halts the book (nothing is adopted without the owner's true-up); the "what still halts" lists of the paper spec's amendments of 2026-10-13 (#1627 item 4), 2026-10-19 (#1762 item 7), 2026-10-20 (#2033 item 4) and 2026-10-26 (#2183 to #2185), for their name-level cases  ·  **Amends:** [ADR 0017](0017-fast-paper-and-machine-readiness-gate.md) part E (E.2 and E.4's real-halt clause; the dated amendment in that record)  ·  **Subsumes:** [#2156](https://github.com/josejuarez96/tradepartner/issues/2156)  ·  **Related:** [ADR 0007](0007-clock-failures-halt-path.md), [ADR 0008](0008-llm-role.md), [ADR 0018](0018-end-user-web-app.md), [ADR 0024](0024-lift-long-only-and-top-1000.md)

## Context

Paper spec req 6 compares the ledger with the broker at the start and end of every run. Anything the journal cannot explain is a `ReconciliationError`: the kill switch engages, the whole book stops trading, and only `paper resume` after an `ok` reconciliation opens it again. Every corporate event the explainers did not yet know (a spin-off's cash in lieu, a reverse split's fraction, a late merger row, a withdrawn announcement, a broker's own fill correction) has therefore stopped every name in the book, and each has become an issue, an amendment and a PR. A `gh` search on 2026-10-26 finds 115 issues mentioning reconciliation opened since 2026-09-26, and the owner's count of event-driven fixes is about 150. The daily book lost a full session to one malformed broker row on 2026-10-21 (#2149), and each whole-book halt also restarts the shakedown span (ADR 0017 E.2 and E.4), so a rare external event costs the daily book days of testing.

Seven issues are open now for single events of this kind: #2184, #2122, #2107, #2147, #1930, #2092 and #2112. #2156 asked which faults could be isolated to one name. On 2026-10-26 the owner chose standard reconciliation practice over further per-event code (#2266).

**Standard practice.** The evidence is secondary and descriptive, grade B at best. It is enough to show what is normal, not to set numbers.

- **The broker or custodian is the book of record.** Our ledger is a copy that is checked against it. QuantConnect LEAN, a widely used open-source engine, loads holdings and open orders from the brokerage when a live algorithm starts ([LEAN docs, "Live Trading > Brokerages"](https://www.quantconnect.com/docs/v2/writing-algorithms/live-trading/brokerages)). It also re-syncs cash with the brokerage once a day instead of modelling every cash event ([QuantConnect forum thread 16567](https://www.quantconnect.com/forum/discussion/16567/force-refresh-binance-brokerage-account-cash-balance/), a staff answer).
- **A difference is a break in an exception queue,** with a size, an age and an owner. Breaks below a materiality threshold are accepted. Aged breaks are escalated. US broker-dealer rules show the aging idea: a short securities difference unresolved 45 days after discovery must be bought in under Rule 15c3-3(h) ([FINRA Notice to Members 00-45](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/00-45)), and net capital is charged on differences unresolved for seven days ([a comment letter on S7-05-14](https://www.sec.gov/comments/s7-05-14/s70514-7.htm), secondary).
- **A restricted list works per security.** One security is blocked for new trades, and the rest of the business goes on.
- **A correction is an adjusting entry,** with a reason, a person and an audit trail. It is reversed by a later entry and never deleted. Domain rule 8 ("log overrides") already says the same for this project.
- **Corporate actions are applied as the broker or vendor reports them,** then checked. They are not modelled per event before the system may trade. Our explainers (splits, dividends, spin-offs, mergers, cash in lieu) stay as a way to clear breaks automatically. They are no longer a gate.

## Options considered

1. **Keep the whole-book halt and fix each event as it comes** (today). Pro: nothing is ever adopted without a rule. Con: about one new issue a day; every rare event stops every name and costs shakedown days; a halted book cannot exit a losing name (the owner, #2156).
2. **Adopt the broker's numbers automatically every run** (LEAN's cash sync, applied to positions as well). Pro: no halts and no owner work. Con: it hides our own bugs, such as a mis-sized order or a lost fill, behind a silent re-base. The journal would stop being a check on the machine, which is what the shakedown measures.
3. **Breaks, restriction and owner true-up** (this record). Pro: the rest of the book keeps trading; small differences are accepted and logged; a material difference on one name blocks only that name until the owner accepts it with a typed reason; account-level faults still stop the book. Con: a schema version, a new owner command, and a new state (an open break) that the owner must clear.

## Decision

We take option 3, for paper books. The live book inherits it only through the Phase 6 live ADR.

1. **The broker is the source of truth for positions and cash.** The ledger is our copy, rebuilt from the journal and checked against the broker at every reconciliation, as today.
2. **A difference the explainers do not clear is a break, not a halt.** A break has a kind, a name (or `cash`), a dollar size, a first-seen session, an age in XNYS sessions and a state. Code computes all of these at detection; nothing comes from an LLM (ADR 0008, domain rule 4).
3. **Materiality comes from config.** A break at or below the materiality threshold is accepted automatically: the reconciliation journals an adjusting entry that adopts the broker's figure and logs it. A material break restricts its name. The thresholds are frozen `risk.*` keys; the paper spec amendment names them and their proposed defaults.
4. **Restrict the name, not the book.** A material break restricts its name: no order may open or add exposure in that name until the break is cleared. Orders that reduce risk in it may be allowed (an open question in the amendment). The rest of the book trades, and the owner is alerted.
5. **The whole-book halt stays for account-level faults,** and for any fault that says the machine, not the market, is wrong:
   - the account id differs;
   - cash that no name accounts for is beyond the cash bound;
   - a foreign, unexplained or altered open order, or a bad order reading (our own order path);
   - the open breaks together pass a cap (many breaks at once suggest a systemic fault, not many events);
   - clock faults, stale data, broker connectivity, limit breaches, the kill switch, a collapsed universe, and the rejection and skip caps, all unchanged (ADR 0007, ADR 0023 D2).
6. **The owner trues up a break** with `paper true-up`. It needs a typed reason (never defaulted) and a fresh broker read. It journals one adjusting entry stamped with a point-in-time `known_at`, and a later entry can reverse it. Reconciliation then treats the name as explained.
7. **The corporate-action explainers stay** (dividends, splits, spin-offs, mergers, cash in lieu, ended listings, ticker changes) and run first. Only what they leave becomes a break. When a later store row lets an explainer clear an open break, the break closes on its own with the explainer's usual adjustment. A missing explainer is no longer a reason to stop the book. One is built only for an event shown to be frequent (#2266's frozen list).
8. **The shakedown span (ADR 0017 part E) restarts only when the machine is at fault.** A break with an external cause is counted and printed. That means a corporate action in the store, or a broker corporate-action or dividend activity on the name, of a type that can produce the break. A late row or a data delay counts only when the broker's activity shows it at detection. A break whose cause is not established, a book-wide halt, and our own order or journal faults still count against the span. Code records the cause at detection, and never changes it afterwards. Where the cause is unclear, the break counts as restarting. The dated amendment in ADR 0017 states the lines.

**Effect on open work** (#2266): #2184, #2122, #2107, #2147, #1930, #2092 and #2112 are handled by restriction and true-up once the build lands, unless they are shown to be frequent. #2185 (the old ticker on the order path) and #2174 (a short open labelled realised) are real defects and stay fixes. One-off reconcile edge-case fixes are frozen until this lands.

## Consequences

- Good: a rare event costs one name and one owner command, not the whole book and days of the shakedown; the per-event amendment loop stops; the journal stays a check on the machine, because a material break is never adopted without the owner's reason; an account-level fault still stops everything.
- Bad / accepted risks: a restricted name can drift from its target while it waits for the owner. A sub-threshold difference is adopted without a person. A true-up can hide a defect of ours if the owner accepts it too quickly; the typed reason, the journal entry and its reversal are the controls. Ledger equity differs from the broker's by the open breaks until they are cleared, bounded by the cap. Paper books stop being a strict test of every corporate-action rule.
- Reversibility: costly but not one-way. A materiality of zero and a break cap of zero turn every break back into a halt. The keys are frozen per window, so this takes effect through `paper stop` and a new window. The true-up entries stay in the append-only journal.
- Revisit if: one break kind recurs often enough to deserve an explainer; the owner finds himself truing up more than about one break a week per book; a true-up is later found to have hidden a defect of ours; or the Phase 6 live ADR needs a stricter rule for real money.
