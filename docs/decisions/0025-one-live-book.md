# 0025. One live book on a taxable cash account

**Status:** Proposed  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-20  ·  **Issue:** #2120  ·  **Amends:** [paper-trading spec](../specs/paper-trading.md)  ·  **Related:** [ADR 0017](0017-fast-paper-and-machine-readiness-gate.md) part F, [ADR 0023](0023-break-first-fast-books-paper-exam.md) D6, [ADR 0024](0024-lift-long-only-and-top-1000.md), [ADR 0005](0005-objective-benchmark-stop-criteria.md) Live amendment, [ADR 0010](0010-phase-4-risk-rules.md) #658 amendment, [charter](../charter.md), [roadmap](../roadmap.md) Phase 6, [plan](../plans/paper-trading.md) T163–T163c

## Context

Phase 4 exits on the machine shakedown, not on a strategy contest ([ADR 0017](0017-fast-paper-and-machine-readiness-gate.md) E–F). On 2026-10-20 its ten-session span has not finished ([shakedown runbook](../runbooks/shakedown.md)); the first kill-ladder verdicts are also pending. This is a draft decision rule, not a declaration that a strategy or the live path is ready. T163 may be accepted only after `paper shakedown` exits 0, the first verdicts (at least B10 rungs 1 and 4) are recorded on their issues, and the taxable cash account exists. The charter's employer check is ticked not applicable; #878 is decided in ADR 0005's 2026-10-18 Live amendment. The owner and `safety-reviewer` still gate the live order path.

Today's code cannot reach live: `src/tradepartner/config.py:AlpacaConfig._guard_paper` refuses `false`; `src/tradepartner/adapters/alpaca_trading_raw.py:AlpacaTradingRaw.__init__` checks the flag, reads a paper pair and checks an injected client's URL; `src/tradepartner/adapters/alpaca_broker.py:AlpacaBroker.__init__` checks it again; `src/tradepartner/execution/brokers.py:build_broker` constructs only that adapter. `src/tradepartner/execution/window.py:_refuse_small_account` checks the start bound, while `src/tradepartner/execution/lots.py:_wash_sale_flags` matches within one security identity. These are deliberate guards, not evidence about a live account.

## Options considered

1. **Separate live store and order path.** Isolates records, but duplicates the journal, wrapper and checks and makes paper/live attribution harder.
2. **A global `alpaca.paper=false` switch.** Small change, but exposes every book and recorder to the live endpoint.
3. **One named live book in the shared store** (chosen). Keeps the existing journal and wrapper with two endpoint guards and distinct credentials; the live book must be explicitly selected and verified.

## Decision

1. **Book and endpoint.** Reserve token `live` for exactly one live account and at most one open window in the existing store and journal. Other tokens remain paper only. `paper.book_id` is not an endpoint switch. A new explicit, default-off live-book setting permits `alpaca.paper=false` only when `book_id == "live"`; `AlpacaConfig._guard_paper`, `AlpacaTradingRaw.__init__` and `AlpacaBroker.__init__` each reject every other combination, including a mismatched injected client URL. `build_broker` routes by that explicit setting and book. `TradingClient(..., paper=False)` receives only `ALPACA_LIVE_API_KEY` and `ALPACA_LIVE_API_SECRET`, never `ALPACA_PAPER_*` or market-data keys; paper books never read the live pair. Missing, blank, reused or cross-endpoint pairs fail before a client is made. Keep the window's broker account-id check; record live endpoint broker facts and scrubbed responses by T48b's method before an order. No generic `--live` or bypass flag. T163b implements this and updates the [scheduling runbook](../runbooks/scheduling.md).
2. **Cash and sizing.** The live account is taxable cash, long-only, no leverage or intraday trade ([ADR 0024](0024-lift-long-only-and-top-1000.md)); a later ADR must change that. T163c adds defaulted `Account.settled_cash` from the broker account read. A live cash-account start refuses when it is unavailable or the broker account type cannot be verified. At start, `window.start` applies the [spec's start feasibility rule](../specs/paper-trading.md) to settled cash. At every buys phase, the wrapper budgets the lesser of reconciled ledger cash less open-buy reservations and broker-reported settled cash less those reservations; same-session sale proceeds are excluded until the broker reports them settled. A missing/stale settled-cash read refuses buys and alerts; it never falls back to `buying_power` or total cash. Paper margin books retain their current cash rule. The account's actual type and whether its `cash` includes unsettled proceeds are recorded by the owner before start, as the [charter](../charter.md) requires. The small-account profile uses fractional notional buys and the frozen risk limits; `n × min_order_notional × (1 + whole_share_price_buffer) ≤ spendable settled cash` must hold at start.
3. **Strategy choice.** The owner fills the decision record in open question 1 *before acceptance*, after the gate above. The eligible set is long-only cash-feasible hypotheses with recorded kill-ladder survival, an exam of record under [ADR 0016](0016-development-boundary-and-forward-exams.md), and a reported tracking check against their **own** backtest. A historical exam must be a valid one-spend holdout result; a forward exam must finish its frozen `paper.min_rebalances` and its own tracking check. Require at least **one scheduler-executed, compared, non-excluded tracking rebalance** for a historical-exam strategy before its first live start; this is a machine-drift screen, not a second exam. A failed tracking bound disqualifies it until investigated and a new recorded decision, never silently waived. The owner chooses among eligible survivors using their exams, predeclared costs and feasibility, records why the others did not qualify, and freezes the selected hypothesis identity, book token, cadence, target-name count, small-account profile and approximate $100 starting equity in the live window. No paper-book ranking or comparison selects it. If nobody qualifies, no live start. This ADR does not yet name a strategy or numeric size.
4. **Stops and duration.** Freeze `paper.tracking_rule`, `paper.tracking_k`, modelled costs, risk limits and the ADR 0005 Live stop rule in the live window. The live residual is measured against that hypothesis's own backtest on the same dates by [paper spec](../specs/paper-trading.md) req 10, not against another paper book. On a non-excluded rebalance whose absolute residual exceeds `tracking_k × modelled cost`, engage the kill switch with `source=tracking`, submit no further order, and require an owner retro before release. Keep the existing fault, drawdown, position, exposure, cash and integrity stops. The first live window runs **six rebalances at its frozen cadence**, then the owner records continue or stop; ADR 0005's six-month phase clock also applies. Capital remains about $100 and is never topped up without a charter amendment; total loss alone is not a new stop.
5. **Tax ledger.** Before the first taxable order, carry lots across windows of the live account, including acquisition dates, basis, split/merger adjustments and wash-sale carry-forwards, without importing paper-account lots as owned by live. Apply [ADR 0010's #658 policy](0010-phase-4-risk-rules.md): same `security_id`, plus same-issuer successor after reorganisation or redomicile; a reused ticker of an unrelated issuer is distinct. Record each successor judgment and its reason, and obtain the tax-adviser confirmation that ADR 0010 requires. The live start refuses until this entry work is complete. T90's 1099-B comparison is read-only, per disposal/lot for proceeds, basis and box 1g; it reports every difference and never rewrites journal rows. Run it when the broker's first taxable-year export exists, with the export's coverage and any unavailable fields stated. Broker fee activity remains a reconciled cash event, not a fill or lot; fee differences appear in the comparison rather than being silently folded into basis.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Which survivor, and with what frozen plan?** After the shakedown and at least B10's rung-1 and rung-4 verdicts, record the chosen hypothesis id and exam result, the tracking report and compared rebalance count, the alternatives and their kill verdicts, the cadence, target names, fractional-notional sizing, feasible settled-cash calculation and actual account id. **Recommendation:** choose the first eligible long-only survivor whose own exam and tracking pass and whose frozen plan fits $100; if none does, leave this ADR Proposed and the live switch disabled. Do not preselect H1, B10 or the daily operations book.
2. **Broker cash facts.** Confirm the opened account is cash, the live API accepts it, and which field reports settled buying cash after a same-day sale; attach scrubbed read evidence. **Recommendation:** require a verified broker settled-cash field and refuse a live start until it is available; do not use the #813 margin fallback without a new decision on this cash rule.
3. **Tax entry evidence.** Which successor mappings, cross-window lots and adviser confirmation are complete before the first order? **Recommendation:** require the three policy cases and a cross-window loss/replacement fixture to pass before enablement; keep the 1099-B real-export run as a dated follow-up after the first tax year.

## Consequences

- Good: one journal and order path serve the live book, with explicit credentials, endpoint and account checks; selection follows the strategy's own exam and tracking evidence.
- Bad / accepted risks: about $100 can be lost, fractional fills and settled cash may differ from paper, and the first 1099-B arrives after the first tax year. The tracking stop and read-only tax comparison expose those differences.
- Reversibility: the endpoint switch can be disabled before any order; after a live fill the journal, lots and tax record must be retained and a stop uses the wrapper's flattening path.
- Revisit if: the broker lacks a usable settled-cash field, a live residual breaches its frozen bound, the account type changes, a successor-security case falls outside #658, or no kill-ladder survivor fits the small account.
