# 0025. One live book on a taxable cash account

**Status:** Proposed  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-20  ·  **Issue:** #2120  ·  **Amends:** [paper-trading spec](../specs/paper-trading.md)  ·  **Related:** [ADR 0017](0017-fast-paper-and-machine-readiness-gate.md) part F, [ADR 0023](0023-break-first-fast-books-paper-exam.md) D6, [ADR 0024](0024-lift-long-only-and-top-1000.md), [ADR 0005](0005-objective-benchmark-stop-criteria.md) Live amendment, [ADR 0010](0010-phase-4-risk-rules.md) #658 amendment, [charter](../charter.md), [roadmap](../roadmap.md) Phase 6, [plan](../plans/paper-trading.md) T163–T163c

## Context

Phase 4 exits on the machine shakedown, not on a strategy contest ([ADR 0017](0017-fast-paper-and-machine-readiness-gate.md) E–F). On 2026-10-20 its ten-session span has not finished ([shakedown runbook](../runbooks/shakedown.md)); the first kill-ladder verdicts are also pending. This is a draft decision rule, not a declaration that a strategy or the live path is ready. T163 may be accepted only after `paper shakedown` exits 0, the first verdicts (at least B10 rungs 1 and 4) are recorded on their issues, and the taxable cash account exists. The charter's employer check is ticked not applicable; #878 is decided in ADR 0005's 2026-10-18 Live amendment. The owner and `safety-reviewer` still gate the live order path.

Today's code cannot reach live: `src/tradepartner/config.py:AlpacaConfig._guard_paper` refuses `false`; `src/tradepartner/adapters/alpaca_trading_raw.py:AlpacaTradingRaw.__init__` checks the flag, reads a paper pair and checks an injected client's URL; `src/tradepartner/adapters/alpaca_broker.py:AlpacaBroker.__init__` checks it again; `src/tradepartner/execution/brokers.py:build_broker` constructs only that adapter. `src/tradepartner/execution/window.py:_refuse_small_account` checks the start bound, while `src/tradepartner/execution/lots.py:_wash_sale_flags` matches within one security identity. These are deliberate guards, not evidence about a live account. At $100 H1's unchanged roughly 100-name plan is infeasible ([paper spec](../specs/paper-trading.md) open question 17); B10's proposed book is a tracking window on a spent momentum holdout, not a new exam of record ([plan](../plans/paper-trading.md) T161); the daily momentum book is an operations file, not a strategy bet (the plan's 2026-10-10 amendment). None is named for live. With the scheduled span ending 2026-11-04 if it passes, **2026-11-05 is only the earliest possible decision date**, not a live-order promise: ladder verdicts, a cash account, a T+1-examined eligible hypothesis, the builds and the owner drill can push the first order later.

## Options considered

1. **Separate live store and order path.** Isolates records, but duplicates the journal, wrapper and checks and makes paper/live attribution harder.
2. **A global `alpaca.paper=false` switch.** Small change, but exposes every book and recorder to the live endpoint.
3. **One named live book in the shared store, with a per-book endpoint** (chosen). Keeps one journal and wrapper while `alpaca.paper` remains guarded true; per-book guards, distinct credentials and the reserved token add build and operating work.
4. **Derive settled cash from the journal and exchange calendar.** Works if the broker omits a settled-cash field, but a missed outside-account activity or fill could overstate spendable cash. Cross-check with the broker's reported cash and activity feed, then take the lower bound. The owner chooses the verified source in open question 2.

## Decision

1. **Book and endpoint.** Reserve token `live` for exactly one live account and at most one open window in the existing store and journal. `alpaca.paper` remains guarded `true` in `src/tradepartner/config.py:AlpacaConfig._guard_paper`; it is not the live switch. Add `trading.book_endpoints.live = "live"` (all other tokens implicitly `paper`) and a separate default-off `trading.live_enabled = false`, validated at Settings construction. The owner alone flips `live_enabled` in his local configuration, records the act in this ADR and freezes the endpoint, enabled state and verified account id in the live window, and holds the keys. `src/tradepartner/execution/brokers.py:build_broker`, `src/tradepartner/adapters/alpaca_trading_raw.py:AlpacaTradingRaw.__init__` and `src/tradepartner/adapters/alpaca_broker.py:AlpacaBroker.__init__` each receive `book_id`, check the per-book endpoint and enable independently, and verify an injected client's base URL. Paper books always construct `TradingClient(paper=True)` and keep running while live is enabled; only `live` constructs `TradingClient(paper=False)`. No call may flip Settings globally or pass an endpoint override. `paper.book_id` is only a default book token, never a switch.
2. **Credentials, identity and drill.** `ALPACA_LIVE_API_KEY` and `ALPACA_LIVE_API_SECRET` are `SecretStr`, documented by name in `.env.example`; only the `live` branch unwraps them. Error and log text names variables, never values; keys never enter a fixture, recorder, journal, LLM input or PR. Compare the unwrapped live key and secret **as strings** with each member of every configured paper pair and the market-data pair; any equality, blank or missing member refuses before client creation. `src/tradepartner/config.py:paper_key_variable_names` refuses `live` rather than resolving `ALPACA_PAPER_BOOKS__LIVE__*`; paper `window.start` refuses token `live`. Live start refuses if **any** pre-existing window row has book `live` but a different account id or a paper endpoint, including a closed row. New windows freeze `trading.endpoint` in `paper_windows.frozen_json`; older rows without it are treated as paper. The first live account read must equal the window's frozen account id on every later read. Live broker facts (`quantity_decimals`, `client_order_id_max_length`, account type and settled-cash source) live under `trading.live_broker`, separate from paper `alpaca.*`, and are recorded from scrubbed live responses before an order. The owner cannot flip `live_enabled` until T163b, T163c and T263–T265 are merged and their tests pass. Before enablement, T163b pins every endpoint/enable/injected-URL/key combination at all three guards; with zero orders on the live window the owner performs one read-only account, positions and open-orders reconciliation that returns `ok`, then an engage → no-order run → owner release drill. The [scheduling runbook](../runbooks/scheduling.md) records this evidence and key rotation. There is no generic `--live` or bypass flag.
3. **Cash and sizing.** The live account is taxable cash, long-only, no leverage or intraday trade ([ADR 0024](0024-lift-long-only-and-top-1000.md)); a later ADR must change that. T163c adds defaulted `Account.settled_cash` and the account-type read. The owner chooses a verified broker field or a journal-derived settled balance in open question 2. Journal derivation starts with reconciled starting settled cash, applies only executed cash activities and fills whose sale trade date is at least one XNYS settlement session old, and subtracts buys and open-buy reservations; it is cross-checked against the broker's cash/activity read and the lower nonnegative balance wins. A mismatch beyond the frozen cash tolerance halts. At start, `window.start` verifies cash account type and applies the [spec's feasibility rule](../specs/paper-trading.md) to spendable settled cash. The live buy budget is `max(0, min(reconciled ledger cash, verified settled cash) − open-buy reservations)`. Same-session sale proceeds cannot fund buys. `trading.live_cash_max_age_seconds` (default 30, frozen in the window) defines stale: the settled-cash and activity read must finish in the current buys phase after its last sell-fill collection, and be no older than that bound at each buy submit; otherwise refuse buys and alert. The start read uses the same bound. Never fall back to buying power or total cash. Paper margin books retain their current cash rule. The small-account profile uses fractional notional buys and the frozen risk limits; `n × min_order_notional × (1 + whole_share_price_buffer) ≤ spendable settled cash` must hold at start.
4. **Strategy choice.** The owner fills open question 1 *before acceptance*, after the gate above. Eligible hypotheses are long-only and cash-feasible, survived their recorded kill ladder, have an exam of record under [ADR 0016](0016-development-boundary-and-forward-exams.md), and have a reported tracking check against their own backtest. A historical exam is a valid one-spend holdout; a forward exam finishes its frozen `paper.min_rebalances` and tracking check. A historical-exam strategy needs at least **one scheduler-executed, compared, non-excluded tracking rebalance**; a failed bound disqualifies it pending a recorded investigation and new decision. Freeze **exactly the examined hypothesis's own** cadence, target-name count, sizing rule and other parameters, plus book `live` and approximately $100 equity. A different cadence, count or sizing is a **new registered hypothesis** that must pass its own ladder, exam and tracking check; small-account rounding may not silently change the examined plan. Select by exams, predeclared costs and feasibility, never paper-book ranking. LLM-derived inputs can reach orders only through the registered deterministic plan and risk rules; LLM output never selects the live strategy or submits/cancels an order. If nobody qualifies, no live start.
5. **Stops and duration.** Freeze `paper.tracking_rule`, `paper.tracking_k`, modelled costs, risk limits and the ADR 0005 Live stop rule in the window. T+1 settlement must be modelled in the selected hypothesis's **registered backtest and exam**: sells release cash next XNYS session, so delayed buys and idle cash enter its returns and the live residual on equal terms. Do not exclude settlement idle cash from the residual. A hypothesis examined without that model is ineligible until re-registered and re-examined (T263). The live residual is against that hypothesis's own backtest on the same dates ([paper spec](../specs/paper-trading.md) req 10). Before the next order, when the last completed non-excluded rebalance period has absolute residual above frozen `tracking_k × modelled cost`, `src/tradepartner/execution/switch.py:engage` records `source=tracking`; no further order is submitted until owner retro and normal release. T264 adds the schema value and automatic run-path check. Existing fault, drawdown, position, exposure, cash and integrity stops continue. The first live window runs **six rebalances at its frozen cadence**, then the owner records continue or stop; ADR 0005's six-month phase clock also applies. Capital remains about $100 and is never topped up without a charter amendment; total loss alone is not a new stop.
6. **Tax ledger.** Before the first taxable order, carry lots across windows of the live account, including acquisition dates, basis, split/merger adjustments and wash-sale carry-forwards, without importing paper-account lots as live holdings. Apply [ADR 0010's #658 policy](0010-phase-4-risk-rules.md): same `security_id`, plus same-issuer successor after reorganisation or redomicile; unrelated ticker reuse is distinct. Record each successor judgment and reason; obtain the tax-adviser confirmation ADR 0010 requires. T265 adds an append-only `owner_decisions.kind=live_tax_clearance` row keyed to the verified live account and policy version, with the adviser-confirmation date and reason; `window.start` refuses until that row and the cross-window ledger/successor checks pass. The row records the owner evidence, not a claim that the adviser approves future cases. T90's 1099-B comparison is read-only per disposal/lot for proceeds, basis and box 1g, reports every difference and never rewrites journal rows; run it when the first taxable-year export exists and state coverage and unavailable fields. Broker fees remain cash events, not fills or lots; report fee differences, not silent basis changes. A realised-P&L/open-lot report is a follow-up, not an entry gate.

## Open questions for the owner

1. **Which survivor, and with what frozen plan?** After the shakedown and at least B10's rung-1 and rung-4 verdicts, record the chosen hypothesis id, its T+1-aware exam and tracking report, the alternatives and their kill verdicts, the hypothesis's **unchanged** cadence, target names and sizing, the feasible settled-cash calculation and actual account id. **Recommendation:** choose the first eligible long-only survivor whose own exam and tracking pass and whose examined plan fits $100; if none does, leave this ADR Proposed and live disabled. Do not preselect H1, B10 or the daily operations book.
2. **Settled-cash source and broker facts.** Confirm the opened account is cash, the live API accepts it, whether its cash includes unsettled proceeds, and whether a timestamped settled-cash field is available after sells; attach scrubbed evidence. **Recommendation:** use the broker field cross-checked against the journal/calendar lower bound if it exists; otherwise use the journal-derived bound only after an account/activity reconciliation confirms no outside cash movement. Refuse start if neither is provable; do not use #813's margin fallback without a new cash-rule decision.
3. **Tax entry evidence.** Which successor mappings, cross-window lots and adviser confirmation are complete before the first order? **Recommendation:** require the three policy cases and a cross-window loss/replacement fixture to pass before enablement; keep the 1099-B real-export run as a dated follow-up after the first tax year.

## Consequences

- Good: one journal and order path serve the live book, with explicit credentials, endpoint and account checks; selection follows the strategy's own exam and tracking evidence.
- Bad / accepted risks: about $100 can be lost; T+1 cash leaves capital idle at a rebalance; live and paper rows share one store and reports must label the endpoint clearly; real-money commands retain the `paper` CLI name. Fractional fills and settled cash may differ from paper, and the first 1099-B arrives after the first tax year. The tracking stop and tax comparison expose differences. The extra per-book guards, T+1 exam and tax entry work delay the first order.
- Reversibility: the endpoint switch can be disabled before any order; after a live fill the journal, lots and tax record must be retained and a stop uses the wrapper's flattening path.
- Revisit if: neither broker nor journal can establish settled cash, a live residual breaches its frozen bound, the account type changes, a successor-security case falls outside #658, or no kill-ladder survivor fits the small account. A realised-P&L and tax-lot report and a key-rotation procedure in the scheduling runbook remain follow-ups; neither changes the first-order gate above.
