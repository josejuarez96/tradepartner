# 0022. No vendor security master: identity stays SEC filings plus Alpaca, measured against a free answer key

**Status:** Accepted (the owner's decision of 2026-10-14 on #1682; his merge of the amendment PR records it)  ·  **Date:** 2026-10-14  ·  **Issue:** #1682 (data-foundation plan T145; amends [ADR 0009](0009-price-vendor.md))

## Context

The store keys every security on its SEC registrant (CIK) and class, and reads tickers, exchanges and titles from cover pages, Forms 25 and the companies snapshot ([spec](../specs/data-foundation.md)). By 2026-10-14 that master carried a dozen inferred rules and lists (the Codex inventory on #1682: primary-id assignment, Form 25 class matching, successor and relisting rules, the snapshot fallback, filename listings, the exchange-axis and split-context readings, title fallback, the resolver's reuse and typo rules, universe rule 2), and the T181 release had needed three parser passes and a restore. The owner asked whether to keep patching or key securities on a real identifier (FIGI, CUSIP or a vendor).

The data-architecture decision record (#1303, [plan amendment #1319](../plans/data-foundation.md)) had left one paid step open: T144, a USD 69 one-month Sharadar TICKERS/ACTIONS overlap test with pass bars written first, and T145, this ADR, to buy or not from its numbers. ADR 0009 holds the running system's data budget at USD 0 and names the triggers that re-open it.

Two read-only measurements on 2026-10-14 changed the question. The Codex review found no rule that a vendor would make unnecessary without a migration of medium to large size (a crosswalk, dated listing events, a bar re-key behind rollback gates), and that a vendor's current snapshot still needs our own `known_at`. The orchestrator's free answer-key check (the S&P 500 membership history at a pinned commit, against `universe_as_of` on 86 month-ends) attributed the 2,232 index member-months we wrongly lose to eleven root causes in our own rules and rows, every one of which is fixable from the evidence we already hold, and found that OpenFIGI's current security type confirms the two causes that concern instrument type. The index key costs nothing and flags every failure type at once.

## Options considered

1. **Buy the vendor master after T144** (the #1303 route): a `permaticker` crosswalk, vendor listing and delisting events, fill-only bars. Pro: one stable identifier per share class, delisted names since 1998, actions with dates. Con: USD 69 now and about USD 499 a year; a medium-to-large migration before any defect is fixed; the vendor's snapshot is current, so a reconstructed universe still needs our acceptance-time stamps; the causes the key found (shares facts, late stamps, an unfollowed rename, a dropped cover-page shape, a sticky F-6 rule) are in our rows and our reads, which a vendor does not touch. Not chosen.
2. **OpenFIGI as the key.** Pro: free, stable through renames and delistings. Con: no effective-dated listing history, so a current lookup written into the past is look-ahead; the same migration cost as option 1 for the crosswalk. Not chosen as a key; kept as a check (below).
3. **Keep patching one name at a time.** Pro: each patch is small. Con: the T181 sitting showed the cumulative cost (three parser versions, a 22,289-bar census that failed, a restore), and the owner's standing rule since 2026-10-14 is that an identity defect is a root-cause rule with a census, never a one-off (memory: no one-off identity rules). Not chosen.
4. **No vendor; a free answer key as a standing check; the root causes fixed in lever order under censuses and named releases** (this ADR). Pro: USD 0; every fix is a rule we can test and a diff the owner agrees to before the store changes; the key measures each release; OpenFIGI's type lookup, dated at its fetch, catches the instrument-type causes going forward. Con: the key covers large caps only (the same causes keep about 27 of the top 1,000 names a month out, a lower bound); two names (FDIC filers) stay out of reach without a vendor; the owner's time goes to two release sittings.

## Decision

We will take option 4.

1. **No vendor.** T144 is not run; T146 to T150 are not built. Whatever Sharadar access the owner holds stays outside the repo (his research library) and never becomes a store source. ADR 0009's budget line (USD 0 a month for the running system) is unchanged, and its triggers stay as written; a vendor re-enters only through a new ADR with the owner's own numbers.
2. **The identity source stays SEC filings plus Alpaca:** CIK and class as the key, tickers and exchanges from filings at their acceptance, the companies snapshot at its fetch, bars and actions from Alpaca, as the spec says. FIGI and CUSIP are not keys.
3. **The free S&P 500 membership history (fja05680/sp500, MIT, pinned by commit SHA) is a standing `health` check** (plan T189, T189b), and **OpenFIGI's current security type is a second check** (T189c), stored with its own fetch-time `known_at`. Both are **checks only**: never a universe, a filter, a signal, a feature or a trial input; a look-ahead test pins that no module outside `health.py` reads either, and that every universe, backtest and paper read is byte-identical with and without them. S&P membership is itself a selection with look-ahead and survivorship in it.
4. **Root cause by root cause, largest lever first, batched into two named releases** (`restate-1682`, `rederive-1682`), each fix a rule behind an existing switch or a read-time function, each behind a **read-only census and an agreed before/after diff** (membership at the key's 86 month-ends, bars and rows touched, trials staled) before any store-wide change. A re-stamp moves a row's `known_at` only to the acceptance of a filing that names the evidence; a row whose evidence was not public stays as it is and the gap is disclosed.
5. **Forward exams stay the norm** (ADR 0016): no longer history is bought, and the development boundary is unchanged.

## Consequences

- Good: no licence, no deletion clause, no crosswalk; every defect becomes a tested rule with a census; the key gives every release one before/after number; the OpenFIGI rows give a dated outside view on instrument type for the next census.
- Bad / accepted risks: the key is large-cap only and the community dataset keys some renamed names by a later ticker (reported as `unresolved`, never silently dropped); the FDIC filers (First Republic, Signature) stay out (claim TP-4); two release sittings stale every registered trial, once each, and the open paper windows restart or are recorded as differing (spec open question 31); the 22 SPAC-era CIKs the #1681 census found are left as they are until a census of their own.
- Reversibility: cheap. Nothing here closes the vendor route; a later ADR can re-open ADR 0009's budget with T144's pass bars as written, and the index and OpenFIGI checks would then measure the vendor the same way they measure us.
- Revisit if: the index check's residual after `rederive-1682` is above fifty member-months for data causes; a registered hypothesis needs history before 2016 or delisted names a filing cannot supply; or a third release of the same kind is needed within a quarter.
