**Book.** Rank scored names descending by signal, break ties by `security_id` ascending, select the first `ceil(0.20 × n_scored)` names, equal weight. Signal at month-end close; trade at the next XNYS session's open. A selected name lacking that open or a month mark is skipped without rank replacement, with equal weight redistributed over executable selected names. Charge **24.2 bp per side on absolute traded notional** for buys and sells. A delisted holding exits at its last traded close and the proceeds stay cash. This is the existing engine's last-close convention, not the unbuilt failure-return shock from amendment #2670.

**Comparators and statistic.** SPY total return uses the store's `prices_daily` bars and split/dividend `actions` through `adjusted_prices_as_of`, with fixed-symbol SPY identity from `store.benchmarks.benchmark_security_ids`. The equal-weighted comparison buys all point-in-time `universe_as_of` members with the same open execution, last-close delisting treatment and 24.2 bp/side traded-notional cost. For each monthly net excess series, `t = mean / (sample standard deviation / sqrt(number of months))`; no HAC or selection correction.

**Feasibility floor, fixed before any return read.** Every signal month must have **at least 20 names in the top quintile**, i.e. `ceil(0.20 × n_scored) ≥ 20` in all 41 months. If any month misses this floor, the verdict is **INSUFFICIENT** and `probe.py --run` must refuse to read returns. A coverage-only dry run establishes this gate.

**Single combine rule after the feasibility floor.** **PASS if and only if all three hold:** (1) mean monthly *net* excess over SPY is strictly positive; (2) its ordinary t statistic is at least 1.0; and (3) mean monthly *net* excess over the equal-weighted universe is strictly positive.
