## Frozen kill line (declared before return evidence)

One month is 20 XNYS sessions. For equal-weight opportunistic officer/director code-P purchases, require positive matched-peer abnormal return net of 15.6 bp per side in the ≥$200M stratum and 24.2 bp per side in all names, with registered t ≥ 1.0 and n_eff ≥ 400 in **each** stratum. All four frozen readings must pass. A smaller effective sample is INSUFFICIENT. The 400 floor is a break-even screen based on assumed 10% residual standard deviation and 50 bp effect, **not a power guarantee**; those assumptions are UNVERIFIED. Exact values are in the pre-registration table below.

## Disconfirmation

- **Searches used:** no live search was possible in this no-network task. I reviewed the repo's [G2 disconfirmation log](2026-09-26-g2-insider-purchases.md#disconfirmation), its full-text summaries of CMP, Oenschläger–Möllenhoff and the modern composite, plus the [claims register](claims.toml) and [ML insider stream](2026-10-03-ml-validation-methodology-brief.md#11-insider-purchase-stream). The earlier G2 work reports 17 searches, nine aimed at disconfirmation; that search is not repeated or independently verified here.
- **Against a pass:** the only direct modern acceptance-timed liquid result is +3 bp gross to the next close, t 0.37; whole-sample day-10 abnormal return is negative. The after-disclosure reaction is front-loaded, and more than 70% of filings in that sample arrived after the close. The modern composite starts at the trade date, admitting pre-disclosure gains. Historical CMP results are from a different disclosure regime and are gross. A general post-publication decay prior and the small-stock concentration add reasons for skepticism, though neither is a signal-specific 20-session estimate.
- **Against a fail:** CMP's pre-SOX filing-safe A4 result and top-half-cap coefficient are positive; routine trades are near zero, suggesting the classification matters. The modern study did not implement CMP or report a liquid one-month cut, so its null at one and ten days cannot prove a null at 20 sessions. Zhao's small-stock CAR is positive, although it does not establish this liquid, net design. No source supplies a valid negative result for the exact frozen line.

## Store feasibility and gaps

The [Form 4 schema](../specs/data-foundation.md) and `src/tradepartner/store/schema.py` place `transaction_code`, `acquired_disposed`, `is_officer`, `is_director`, `shares`, `price`, `issuer_cik`, `filer_cik`, `line_no`, `accepted_at` and `known_at` on `sec_form4_transactions`. `sec_filing_events` supplies `accession`, `form`, `subject_cik` (the Form 4 issuer), `filer_cik`, `accepted_at` and `known_at`. Both enforce `known_at = accepted_at`; the stamp comes from the accession's submissions acceptance, subject to clock and SGML checks. `transaction_date` and `deemed_execution_date` are not event clocks. The store **does not carry the 10b5-1 checkbox**, so flagged trades cannot be excluded and this exclusion remains an explicit limitation. It also lacks the officer title and a private-versus-exchange purchase marker. The as-of reader is feature-gated until the owner closes the stamp risk.

The supplied backfill snapshot has **2,697,584 transaction rows, 2016-04 to 2026-10, about 70% of the selected bulk population, with a second run pending; 338 clock-suspect and 226 issuer-mismatch rows**. These figures are **UNVERIFIED** in this checkout: no real-store query was authorized or available. They are all-transaction totals, not code-P officer/director event counts; they cannot establish the 400-effective-event floor. Incomplete selection can remove an insider's previous-year trade and flip a CMP label, even for a later event; clock-suspect rows cannot be used for entry until repaired; issuer mismatches may misassign events or peers. Missing early history is structural for a three-year classifier. The event-study runner can take a registered event dataset and has `next_open_after_instant`, as-of fact reads, matching, costs, overlap and delisted-exit counts, but the completed SHA-pinned dataset, registered adapter, and counts-only feasibility report must precede the approved return run. The existing [C1 result](2026-10-28-c1-filing-delay-results.md) and [C1b golden](../experiments/c1b-filing-delay-matched.md) show why: SPY comparison was confounded, and the older bar coverage for delisters was poor.

## Caveats & gaps

- `P` cannot isolate open-market trades from private purchases; the requested exact population is not identifiable in v1. The 10b5-1 flag is absent. A strict interpretation of the frozen definition is therefore currently infeasible, independent of whether the return effect exists.
- The published figures in the table are taken from repo reviews. No paper, underlying table or current replication was independently fetched in this task. Dates and key figures labelled UNVERIFIED need source rechecking before they can anchor a registration.
- The floor of 400 effective events is a break-even screening assumption, not a formal power calculation. Same-date and issuer clusters, 20-session overlap, and the missing-issuer bar rate may make the effective count much smaller than the raw Form 4 count. The first feasibility read must state those reductions and the number of delisted issuers with usable bars through the development boundary.
- The event-study runner's last-close rule does not include a failure delisting return. Apply the frozen coverage gate: a numerical pass with poor coverage is pass on survivor data and awaits owner review before rung 3; a numerical kill is killed on survivor data.

## UNVERIFIED items

The supplied backfill counts and gap counts; actual code-P officer/director counts, CMP-classifiable counts and effective n by liquidity stratum; 20-session residual standard deviation and 50 bp plausible net effect; source-level Jeng figures and modern large-cap extension details; actual next-open execution cost including any submission drift; delisted-issuer bar coverage.
