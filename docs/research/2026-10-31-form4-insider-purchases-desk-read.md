# Form 4 insider purchases: candidate 3 desk read

**Brief:** #2673 · **Date:** 2026-10-31 · **Status:** COMPLETE (desk read; no store return run) · **Agent/model:** team codexform4, Codex · **Declared:** 2026-10-31 04:39 UTC, before return-evidence review

## Frozen kill line (declared before return evidence)

One month means 20 trading sessions, bought at the next session open after the verified Form 4 acceptance and exited at the twentieth session close. For equal-weighted, officer/director, open-market code-P purchases classified as opportunistic using only filings already accepted at that event's acceptance, require a positive post-acceptance abnormal return versus causal matched peers **net of both sides** of rung-1 costs and a cluster-robust **t ≥ 1.0**, separately in (1) the universe with trailing 20-session median raw close × volume ≥ $200M ADV, using **15.6 bp per side (31.2 bp round trip)**, and (2) **all names**, using **24.2 bp per side (48.4 bp round trip)**. The two strata must both pass; no result in one substitutes for the other. Require **at least 400 effective independent issuer-event observations in each stratum** for a readable kill, a provisional power floor from assumed 10% one-month residual standard deviation and 50 bp plausible net effect, `n_eff = (0.10 / 0.005)^2`; both inputs are **UNVERIFIED**, and raw rows must be discounted for issuer, acceptance-date and overlapping-hold clustering. A smaller sample yields INSUFFICIENT, not a failure. This is a desk gate, not a reported store result.

## Answer

**Verdict: INSUFFICIENT** · **Confidence:** high that the published evidence cannot meet the frozen line; low about the effect in this store. No DRAFT registration is warranted. The closest acceptance-timed modern study stops at ten days and does not apply the Cohen–Malloy–Pomorski (CMP) classifier. CMP's positive one-month result predates 2008, is gross, and does not use our next-open entry. No source supplies the required post-acceptance 20-session matched abnormal return, cluster-robust t, and net costs in either frozen universe. This does not establish that the effect is zero; it leaves the frozen gate undecidable. The store's event and delister counts have not been read here, so the 400-effective-event feasibility gate also remains open.

## Design held fixed for this read

- Select non-derivative Form 4 rows with `transaction_code = 'P'`, `acquired_disposed = 'A'`, `shares > 0`, non-null positive `price`, and `is_officer OR is_director`. The SEC P code includes **open-market or private purchases**; the store has no field separating them, so this is a code-P proxy, not a verified open-market-only sample. Exclude 4/A revisions from the primary event set; report them separately, because the store has no inferred original link.
- At each filing's `known_at`, label an insider only if that insider has at least one accepted trade in **each** of the previous three calendar years. Routine means a trade in the same calendar month in all three years; otherwise opportunistic. Assign from filings accepted by that instant only, with no later annual relabeling. Unclassifiable insiders are excluded and counted, not called opportunistic. With history starting 2016-04, 2016–2018 cannot have complete three-calendar-year history, and 2019 is the earliest possible test year for sufficiently observed insiders. This is the CMP rule adapted to an acceptance-time information set; a missing backfilled trade can alter the label.
- For a filing with several purchase lines by one insider, sum shares and `shares × price` for diagnostics, but give the insider one vote. For several insiders buying one issuer on one acceptance session, keep one **issuer × entry-session** event, average insider signals without multiplying the return observation, and report the distinct-insider count as a cluster diagnostic. Distinct filings for one issuer entering on the same session coalesce; later sessions remain separate, with overlapping holds reported. Equal weight per issuer-event after coalescing. Cluster inference by issuer and entry-date cohort, with a 20-session overlap adjustment; use the most conservative valid t. Report effective count after the same dependence corrections. The timestamp edge is the first XNYS open strictly after the verified acceptance instant, including same-day open only for pre-open acceptances; an after-open or after-hours filing enters at the following open.
- Abnormal return is the stock's dividend-adjusted entry-open to twentieth-session-close return minus an equal-weight peer return over the same horizon. Peers must be same-session, comparable size and share class, with facts available at the event's acceptance; exclude same issuer. A beta-adjusted matched reading is a required robustness check. SPY alone is not an acceptable small-stock control: C1's SPY result was confounded by size and beta, and C1b's matching rule is the relevant precedent. The peer leg is treated as costless in the kill arithmetic, which flatters a pass. Missing bars and delistings must be counted; last-close-only delisting treatment is optimistic for failures.
- Apply the [ADR 0023 rung-1 convention](../decisions/0023-break-first-fast-books-paper-exam.md#amendment-2026-10-30-2667-one-rung-1-cost-rule): arrival-based, equal-order-weight, book-liquidity-band, rebalance-day paper costs. The +15.6 and +24.2 bp figures are supplied for this frozen desk line. The underlying [paper cost report](2026-10-30-paper-fill-cost-arrival.md) actually gives +10.6 bp per side for over-$200M orders; +15.6 is its **buy mean** in that band. This discrepancy makes our 31.2 bp round trip conservative relative to that report's +21.2 bp band mean. The paper sample is three sessions, with no live-fill validation. An entry at the next open may incur open-to-submission drift beyond the arrival measure, so net returns based on this cost could still be optimistic.

## Disconfirmation

- **Searches used:** no live search was possible in this no-network task. I reviewed the repo's [G2 disconfirmation log](2026-09-26-g2-insider-purchases.md#disconfirmation), its full-text summaries of CMP, Oenschläger–Möllenhoff and the modern composite, plus the [claims register](claims.toml) and [ML insider stream](2026-10-03-ml-validation-methodology-brief.md#11-insider-purchase-stream). The earlier G2 work reports 17 searches, nine aimed at disconfirmation; that search is not repeated or independently verified here.
- **Against a pass:** the only direct modern acceptance-timed liquid result is +3 bp gross to the next close, t 0.37; whole-sample day-10 abnormal return is negative. The after-disclosure reaction is front-loaded, and more than 70% of filings in that sample arrived after the close. The modern composite starts at the trade date, admitting pre-disclosure gains. Historical CMP results are from a different disclosure regime and are gross. A general post-publication decay prior and the small-stock concentration add reasons for skepticism, though neither is a signal-specific 20-session estimate.
- **Against a fail:** CMP's pre-SOX filing-safe A4 result and top-half-cap coefficient are positive; routine trades are near zero, suggesting the classification matters. The modern study did not implement CMP or report a liquid one-month cut, so its null at one and ten days cannot prove a null at 20 sessions. Zhao's small-stock CAR is positive, although it does not establish this liquid, net design. No source supplies a valid negative result for the exact frozen line.

## Store feasibility and gaps

The [Form 4 schema](../specs/data-foundation.md) and `src/tradepartner/store/schema.py` place `transaction_code`, `acquired_disposed`, `is_officer`, `is_director`, `shares`, `price`, `issuer_cik`, `filer_cik`, `line_no`, `accepted_at` and `known_at` on `sec_form4_transactions`. `sec_filing_events` supplies `accession`, `form`, `subject_cik` (the Form 4 issuer), `filer_cik`, `accepted_at` and `known_at`. Both enforce `known_at = accepted_at`; the stamp comes from the accession's submissions acceptance, subject to clock and SGML checks. `transaction_date` and `deemed_execution_date` are not event clocks. The store **does not carry the 10b5-1 checkbox**, so flagged trades cannot be excluded and this exclusion remains an explicit limitation. It also lacks the officer title and a private-versus-exchange purchase marker. The as-of reader is feature-gated until the owner closes the stamp risk.

The supplied backfill snapshot has **2,697,584 transaction rows, 2016-04 to 2026-10, about 70% of the selected bulk population, with a second run pending; 338 clock-suspect and 226 issuer-mismatch rows**. These figures are **UNVERIFIED** in this checkout: no real-store query was authorized or available. They are all-transaction totals, not code-P officer/director event counts; they cannot establish the 400-effective-event floor. Incomplete selection can remove an insider's previous-year trade and flip a CMP label, even for a later event; clock-suspect rows cannot be used for entry until repaired; issuer mismatches may misassign events or peers. Missing early history is structural for a three-year classifier. The event-study runner can take a registered event dataset and has `next_open_after_instant`, as-of fact reads, matching, costs, overlap and delisted-exit counts, but an event builder, classified counts by year and universe, bar/delister coverage, and a causal peer pool still need a read-only feasibility pass before registration. The existing [C1 result](2026-10-28-c1-filing-delay-results.md) and [C1b golden](../experiments/c1b-filing-delay-matched.md) show why: SPY comparison was confounded, and the older bar coverage for delisters was poor.

## Caveats & gaps

- `P` cannot isolate open-market trades from private purchases; the requested exact population is not identifiable in v1. The 10b5-1 flag is absent. A strict interpretation of the frozen definition is therefore currently infeasible, independent of whether the return effect exists.
- The published figures in the table are taken from repo reviews. No paper, underlying table or current replication was independently fetched in this task. Dates and key figures labelled UNVERIFIED need source rechecking before they can anchor a registration.
- The floor of 400 effective events is a screening assumption, not a formal power calculation. Same-date and issuer clusters, 20-session overlap, and the missing-issuer bar rate may make the effective count much smaller than the raw Form 4 count. The first feasibility read must state those reductions and the number of delisted issuers with usable bars through the development boundary.
- The event-study runner's last-close rule does not include a failure delisting return. A positive survivor-only result would be fragile; a negative result would be recorded as a kill on survivor data, as with C1b, until the owner rules on retirement.
