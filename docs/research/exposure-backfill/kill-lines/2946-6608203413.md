### Ports and legs
- High (favourable, Sign +1) = `10` in VW, `05` in the screen file; low = `01`.
- L/S = CZ's `LS` port. **Check:** in every month of a cell, |`LS` − (high − `01`)| ≤ 1×10⁻⁶ percentage points; if not, the cell is INSUFFICIENT.
- Long = high − equal mean of all decile/quintile ports (10 in VW, 5 in screen) that month. Short = that mean − `01`.

### Cells (six decision cells)
2 files (VW, NYSE-p20 screen) × 3 windows **2009-01..2025-12 (204 months), 2016-01..2025-12 (120), 2020-01..2025-12 (72)**. A cell is INSUFFICIENT if any needed port is missing or null in any month of the cell.

### Statistics and floors
- Mean %/month; **t = mean / (sample sd with ddof 1 / √n)**, ordinary, no HAC.
- Cost base (as #2828 except turnover): **100% one-sided turnover per leg per year**, each unit of turnover two trades, borrow **30 bp/year** on the short leg, **2× margin**.
  - Screen at **25 bp/side**: long cost floor 0.0833, short **0.1333**, L/S **0.2167** %/month.
  - VW at **15 bp/side**: long 0.050, short **0.100**, L/S **0.150** %/month.
- A cell passes only if **all** of: L/S mean ≥ the file's L/S floor (0.2167 screen, 0.150 VW); short mean ≥ the file's short floor (0.1333 screen, 0.100 VW); long mean ≥ +0.10 and short mean ≥ +0.10; t of L/S, long and short each ≥ 1.0. Equality passes; a strict shortfall of any unrounded value fails.
- **Verdict: PASS only if all six cells pass. FAIL if any comparable cell fails. INSUFFICIENT only if no cell fails but at least one cannot be evaluated.** No substitute file, signal, window or floor.

### Reported only, never changes the verdict
