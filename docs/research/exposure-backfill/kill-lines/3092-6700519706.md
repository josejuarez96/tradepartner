**Interpretations of the frozen text.** In each case I took the first-stated or literal reading and will not try alternatives for the verdict.
1. *Costs.* Long leg: 2 sides x 120%/yr x bp per side / 12, so 3.0 bp a month VW and 5.0 bp screen. It pays no borrow; the short side of "top minus mean of ports" is treated as the benchmark, as in the desk reads. L/S: two legs plus 30 bp/yr borrow, so 8.5 / 12.5 bp a month. 2x cost doubles every component.
2. *"Sum of components' turnover".* Read as the weight-sum (each component's 120% on its 1/N weight), so the composite pays 120% a year with no netting credit. Yearly re-formation turnover is not added.
3. *Long leg.* Top port minus the equal mean of the ports present that month (top, 01 and LS required). Two VW signals (PS, DelLTI) have middle ports that exist only in some months. LS = top − 01 is checked to 1e-6 for every signal, and the run fails on a mismatch.
4. *Eligibility per file.* Arm 3 in a file is 1/N over the pool signals present in that file with SampleEndYear < Y. Each month averages the eligible signals that have a long-leg value that month.
5. *Arm 1 gate.* A replica of rung-2 as practised: both files jointly, each of LS, long and short over the trailing 204/120/72 months ending at Y−1 December. Each needs gross mean ≥ the #2828 base floors (VW 0.1748/0.10/0.1124, screen 0.2436/0.10/0.1468) and t ≥ 1. The same admitted set is used in both files.
6. *Arm 2 gate.* Per file: the t of the net (1x) long leg over its full history to Y−1 must be ≥ 1. Survivors are then admitted greedily in descending t if |ρ| ≤ 0.30 against every one already admitted, using pairwise trailing correlation. #2888's max-8 / min-4 counts are not applied, since the frozen text names only the cap.
7. *An empty arm-year* holds the benchmark (return 0, no cost).
8. *2x cost* re-prices the same admitted sets; gates are not re-run at 2x.
9. *Supplementary calibration.* Synthetic series: demeaned, then circular-block-bootstrapped with 12-month blocks to 432 months, as in step 1. A pure block permutation of the full span would leave every full-span mean fixed and give arm 2 no variance (seen in the dry run). Each seed has 400 signals, 20% planted on the long leg only at net IR 0.3/0.5/0.8; there are 50 seeds. The panel holds the pool signals complete over 1990-2025 in both files.
