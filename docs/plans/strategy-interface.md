# Plan: Strategy interface (strategies as registered objects)

**Spec:** none; the governing document is [ADR 0014](../decisions/0014-strategies-as-registered-objects.md) (size M, development-process "Right-size the ceremony": an ADR for the hard-to-reverse part, the schema CHECK and the paper path, and this plan for the build order); the [backtest spec](../specs/backtest.md)'s Series and data-model lines and the [paper-trading spec](../specs/paper-trading.md)'s `signals` line carry dated notes from this PR  ·  **Status:** Approved (owner, by merging PR #1077; the six owner decisions of 2026-10-06 are in the ADR; its two open questions gate T130 and nothing else)  ·  **Issue:** #1074  ·  **Related plans:** [backtest](backtest.md) (T85 to T85f; T85e's line is amended by this PR), [strategy-lab](strategy-lab.md) (T97, T98, T98b, T100, T105)

## Approach (short)

**Spike first, then planned; size M, not L.** The planner spike of 2026-10-06 (ADR 0014, Context) put a trivial second strategy through `engine.run`, `run_hypothesis`, the T40 invariance suites and the paper `plan_rebalance` step on the fixture store: 13 tests, +31/−9 lines in four source files (a 22-line planner seam in three of them and nine lines of family tables in `config.py`), no schema change, the engine core untouched. It stopped at exactly one boundary, the exclusion reason, which lives in `Plan`, a `NOT NULL` count column and the paper `signals.reason` CHECK. Everything else it found was bookkeeping (the family tables) or silent (the missing dispatch, the momentum fallbacks, the unread cadence, and MTUM wired in by name where the charter wants it). So this plan is **four build tasks and one split** (T127 and T127b, T128, T129, T130), each the smallest change that fixes one finding, plus one amendment to an existing task: the dispatch seam is **T85e's job**, not a new task, because T85e is the first PR that has to dispatch at all and the spike showed that a `signal_for(family)` inside the engine without the family threaded in from `run_hypothesis` and `plan_rebalance` would be a seam that dispatches nothing (every caller still momentum: `cli.py:823`, `execution/run.py:1824`, `execution/report.py:891`). Making T85e the seam keeps one seam; a second task that rewrote T85e's dispatch would leave two for a while and edit `engine.py` twice. The amendment (recorded in the backtest plan's "Amendment 2026-10-06 (#1074)" note) adds to T85e: the family parameter on `engine.run` and `engine.plan`, passed from `run_hypothesis` and `_plan_at`; `signal_for` in a new leaf module `backtest/strategies.py` rather than inside `engine.py`, returning one record (reads, signal, exclusion reasons, count names, where the construction parameters live) that `_plan` follows with no per-family branch; one `Plan.counts` mapping with a default, in place of six named plan fields (T85d's six `RebalanceRow` columns filled from it by name; momentum's one count `n_excluded_no_history` declared); and owner decision 2's `PAPER_FAMILIES` gate.

**Order, from the spike.** (1) The seam (T85e, after T98 and T98b as the backtest plan already orders it, so the family dispatch is written once over the cadence-aware signal call). (2) Beside each other, on disjoint files: the generic exclusions and counts in the engine and the store (T127, the one schema boundary, after T85d so it migrates the six columns rather than racing them, owner decision 6) and the family registry with the fallbacks removed (T128). (3) The paper planner reads the generic plan (T127b, after T127; `execution/` only, `safety-reviewer`), and per-family benchmarks (T129) after T128 (the benchmark is a registry attribute) and after the lab's T97 (the one period-key rename edits the same `metrics.py` and `results.py` lines; two renames in flight is what the lab spec forbids). (4) The proof: B4 `combined` through the registry (T130), which by ADR 0014 point 6 must touch none of `execution/`, `store/`, `engine.py`, `run.py`, `results.py` or `hypothesis.py`; the PR body shows the diff's file list as its evidence. T130 does not wait for T127b: `combined` is not paper-ready, so the paper planner never sees it.

**The latent cadence bug** (spike: `schedule.rebalance_cadence` and `schedule.signal_anchor` frozen and hashed but read nowhere; a `week_end` registration ran monthly and wrote `ok`) is the strategy-lab plan's T98 (engine, holdout and run path at cadence) and T100 (`paper start` refuses non-monthly). This plan does not fix it; every task here that touches `engine.run` sits after T98 by dependency (T85e → T127), so the seam and the cadence loop are never edited at once. The lab's T105 (`run_many`; `run` becomes `run_many` with one variant) rewrites `engine.run`'s body too and depends on T98 only: it is **not** disjoint from T85e's `family` threading or T127's `Plan` fields, so the rule for `engine.py` is the chain T98 → T85e → T127 plus T105, whichever of T105 and the chain's current task lands later merges `main` first and keeps the dispatch through `signal_for` and the `counts`/`exclusions` fields intact (its PR says so). Adding T85e to T105's dependencies would push the lab's chain past rule 2's bound, so the rule is stated here instead of on T105's line.

**Identity pins in every task.** `H1_FINGERPRINT` and `TWIN_FINGERPRINT` (`tests/backtest/test_frozen_defaults.py:182-186`) are named in every task's Tests and asserted unchanged; the H1 fixture's metric keys and values are asserted unchanged by T129; the momentum fixture plan's `signals` rows are asserted byte-identical by T127b; a `strategy.*` live value changing neither a `combined` run nor its hash is asserted by T130.
